# Profile: header, ledger, rank, trophies, activity

Scope: the Profile page (`ui/profile/` ProfileScreen, ProfileHeader, ProfileSurfaceSections, ProfileActivityMonth/Year, ProfileExtras, ProfileChrome, ProfileTiles, ProfileCharts, ProfileLedger, ProfileSkeleton, ProfileViewModel, `state/`), the avatar sheet and defaults, and the rank, trophy and rank-card files, which are behind `Features.SHOW_GAMIFICATION = false` and wait-listed by SETTLED, so nothing is proposed for them. 17 raised, 14 kept (11 revised), 3 dropped.

The page is close to doctrine. Its debt comes from drift that nothing caught. The BODY rows have moved away from what DESIGN §8 and MAP describe: they lost their action pill and their goal line, round the weight figure, and don't name their delta windows. They also don't fit at 200% font scale, and Profile has no screenshot goldens at any scale, so none of this was flagged. The identity cover draws the name in Material's stock sans, and the only way to rename is a tap target nested inside the photo's tap target. ACTIVITY is meant to be a calendar you can read a date off, but it has no day numbers and no marker for today, and its streak counts different days from the squares above it.

## Findings

### PRO-02 · BODY rows with data no longer say what a tap does, so Log, Sync and Open look the same
discoverability · impact medium · effort S · Profile → BODY rows (WEIGHT, BODY FAT, LEAN MASS, WAIST/SIZES)

- **Now:** `BodyMetricRow` makes the whole row one `bounceCombinedClick`, drawn as glyph badge, label, sparkline and figure ([ProfileSurfaceSections.kt:297-307](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L297-L307)). Once a metric has data, nothing on the row names an action ([ProfileSurfaceSections.kt:337-362](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L337-L362)). Only the empty state prints a verb, as plain `bodySmall` text such as "Log your first" ([ProfileSurfaceSections.kt:363-374](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L363-L374)). The four rows behave differently. WEIGHT and BODY FAT open log sheets ([ProfileScreen.kt:346-357](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileScreen.kt#L346-L357)). LEAN MASS starts a Health Connect read on the tap, with no sheet ([ProfileSurfaceSections.kt:268-270](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L268-L270), [LeanMassViewModel.kt:49](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/LeanMassViewModel.kt#L49)). SIZES pushes Measurements ([ProfileSurfaceSections.kt:465-468](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L465-L468)). DESIGN §8 names "the Profile BODY rows' Log/Sync/Open" as its `ForgeRowPill` example ([DESIGN.md:209-216](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L209-L216)), and MAP still documents the pill on these rows ([MAP.md:343-345](../../../.claude/design/MAP.md#L343-L345)). The shared pill ([Capsules.kt:104](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/Capsules.kt#L104)) is used on Coach and Freestyle but not here. No SETTLED or DECISIONS entry records its removal.
- **Why it matters:** For someone who weighs in every day, the page that owns bodyweight shows a readout, not a place to log. Three different behaviours look identical. Tapping LEAN MASS starts a sync the user did not knowingly ask for.
- **Proposal:** Draw `ForgeRowPill(metric.action)` at the end of every row, populated or empty (Log · Log · Sync · Open). The row stays the only tap target. Replace the zero-state text with the same pill, so both states share one shape. Keep `zeroLabel` as the row's TalkBack verb. The pill needs about 52dp, and at 360dp label + sparkline + figure + pill don't fit on one line. To make room, move the figure and its delta under the label on the left rail, the two-rail row MAP documents ([MAP.md:339-345](../../../.claude/design/MAP.md#L339-L345)), and let the sparkline take the space between the rails. Revised: the layout move is added. Reporting the LEAN MASS sync result is dropped from this finding because it is already open as the 2026-09-26 audit's P3 "Silent failures" ([03-profile.md](../2026-09-26/03-profile.md)).
- **Doctrine:** §2③ and §8 specify a whole-row target with a drawn outlined pill for this exact surface. An outlined pill on each row is not the FAILURES "Button wall", which is filled capsules. The pill is drawn, not clickable, so there is no "Fake tap / nested tap". The glyph badges stay (Antho 2026-08-22, "the icons stayed"). Nothing in SETTLED is re-added.

### PRO-07 · The WEIGHT figure is rounded to a whole kg or lb, while its delta and the rows beside it show tenths
ui-polish · impact medium · effort S · Profile → BODY → WEIGHT row

- **Now:** For kg and lb the figure is `display.lastOrNull()?.roundToInt()` ([ProfileSurfaceSections.kt:396-397](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L396-L397)). Only stones goes through `formatWeight`, which was fixed after the 2026-09-26 audit's P2. BODY FAT and LEAN MASS print one decimal ([ProfileSurfaceSections.kt:413](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L413), [:432](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L432)), and the delta under WEIGHT also prints one decimal ([ProfileSurfaceSections.kt:354](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L354)).
- **Why it matters:** During a cut, 81.4 kg and 81.0 kg both read "81" while the line under it says "↓ 0.4", so the figure contradicts its own delta. A week of kg progress can disappear into the rounding. The log sheet shows tenths.
- **Proposal:** For kg and lb, take the figure from `weightInputValue(lb, unit)` ([WeightFormatter.kt:130](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/units/WeightFormatter.kt#L130)). It gives one decimal, prints whole values bare, and always uses a '.' separator. Keep the unit label split off as the row does today.
- **Doctrine:** §11 requires weights to go through `WeightFormatter`. This fixes a figure and its own delta disagreeing on one row, the problem FAILURES "Two units, one section" names. The layout is unchanged.

### PRO-04 · The cover name is drawn in Material's stock sans, not the serif hero voice, and gets cut off at large font sizes
consistency · impact medium · effort S · Profile → cover name

- **Now:** `ForgeTypography` defines `displayLarge` and the headline rungs but no `displaySmall` ([Type.kt:32-70](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/theme/Type.kt#L32-L70)), so `typography.displaySmall` falls back to M3's stock 36sp sans. The name and its edit field both use it ([ProfileHeader.kt:191](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileHeader.kt#L191), [:202-209](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileHeader.kt#L202-L209)), with `maxLines = 1` and an ellipsis (allowlisted at [design-allowlist.txt:182](../../../forge-android/app/src/test/resources/design-allowlist.txt#L182)) and no 1.3× hero clamp. - **Why it matters:** The most personal element on the page uses a different type voice from the page. At 200% font scale the name renders at 72sp and cuts off mid-word ("Alexand…").
- **Proposal:** Render the name through `HeroHeadline` with `style = typography.headlineLarge.copy(shadow = NameShadow)` ([HomeSurfaceCards.kt:27-44](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/components/HomeSurfaceCards.kt#L27-L44)). With callers on Home and Profile it belongs beside `HeroFigure` in `ui/experiment/SurfaceKit.kt`. Give the name field the same style (it moves into the sheet under PRO-03), then remove both `ProfileHeader` max-lines allowlist entries. `CardioSessionDetailSheet.kt:132` hits the same `displaySmall` fallback; pass that to the cardio owner.
- **Doctrine:** §6: page titles are serif ([DESIGN.md:150](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L150)). SETTLED 2026-08-22, "The sans hero", recorded the same defect and the same fix on Home ([SETTLED.md:301](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L301)). §14 bans `maxLines = 1` on names ([DESIGN.md:341](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L341)). The DstIn mask, its stops and the haloes are untouched.

### PRO-09 · The ACTIVITY calendar has no day numbers, no marker for today, and no quick way back to this month
ui-polish · impact medium · effort S · Profile → ACTIVITY grid and month stepper

- **Now:** The cells are bare ~42dp squares with no day-of-month ([ProfileActivityMonth.kt:283-323](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L283-L323)), although the file header says the full-width grid "buys a calendar you can actually read a date off" ([ProfileActivityMonth.kt:72-76](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L72-L76)). Today has no marker. If today has no session it draws in the same rest tone as yesterday, and only the dimmer future tone after it sets it apart ([ProfileActivityMonth.kt:292-297](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L292-L297), [:132-135](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L132-L135)). Home's week strip marks today by drawing its letter in onBg ([SurfaceKit.kt:414](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/experiment/SurfaceKit.kt#L414)). After paging back N months, getting back takes N taps on "Later month", and the month label is plain text ([ProfileActivityMonth.kt:240-249](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L240-L249)).
- **Why it matters:** To answer "what did I do on the 12th", the question the grid was widened for, you have to count squares down from the weekday header. Finding today means reading a small difference in brightness. Comparing with last year costs 12 taps back.
- **Proposal:** Draw the day-of-month number in `labelSmall` in each in-month cell. Use muted on rest and future cells. On lit cells choose onBg or bg by the cell colour's luminance, the same flip `onPrimary` uses (§5). Mark today by setting its number in onBg at a heavier weight, the week strip's idiom. Don't use a ring for today, so it can't be confused with PRO-05's cardio ring. When `monthsBack > 0`, make the month label `clickableLabeled("Back to this month")`. Revised: today's marker changed from a ring to type weight. Show it to Antho on a device first, because the numbers add texture to a grid he tuned.
- **Doctrine:** The heat grid stays the mark (§2②). The numbers label it, they are not prose. Today is told apart by weight, not brightness alone (FAILURES "Invisible ghost"). The 2026-08-31 stepper and the tap-a-lit-day behaviour are kept.

### PRO-05 · ACTIVITY's streak counts only gym sessions, but the grid above it also lights cardio days
consistency · impact medium · effort M · Profile → ACTIVITY grid and readings line

- **Now:** `buildActivityByDay` adds gym sessions and cardio entries into one count per day ([ProfileRepository.kt:340-353](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/ProfileRepository.kt#L340-L353)), so a cardio-only day lights at the same shade as a lifting day. DAY STREAK comes from `StatsRepository.currentStreakDays`, which reads gym finish times only and bridges vacation and planned rest ([StatsRepository.kt:249-291](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsRepository.kt#L249-L291)). BEST comes from `TrophyRepository.computeMaxStreak`, also gym only ([TrophyRepository.kt:191-207](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/TrophyRepository.kt#L191-L207)). MAP sets the grid deliberately to "showed up" across gym and cardio.
- **Why it matters:** A lifter who swaps Wednesday's session for a run sees an unbroken run of lit squares with "2 DAY STREAK" printed under it. The section looks wrong without looking broken. The grid also can't answer "did I lift that day or just run" without a tap on every cell.
- **Proposal:** Return two counts per day from `buildActivityByDay`, gym and cardio. Both lists are already loaded there. Shade the fill from the gym count, and draw a cardio-only day as an unfilled 1.5dp accent ring. Filled squares then match the streak's days, and runs stay visible. Add a ring swatch labelled CARDIO to the key beside REST. Let the cell's spoken reading say "1 cardio session" or "1 session". Revised: SESSIONS now counts what the fill counts (gym sessions), and ACTIVE DAYS counts filled and ringed days (showed up, as MAP has it). Leave the app-wide streak definition alone, because Home and the streak reminder share it.
- **Doctrine:** This fixes FAILURES "Two units, one section". The ring tells the two kinds of day apart by shape, not brightness (FAILURES "Invisible ghost"). MAP's gym-plus-cardio grid is kept. It reuses the 2026-09-28 planned-rest key idiom.

### PRO-13 · At 200% font the BODY row cannot fit, and Profile has no screenshot goldens at any scale
accessibility · impact medium · effort M · Profile → BODY rows; Profile in CI

- **Now:** `BodyMetricRow` is a single row that never wraps: a 34dp badge, a label of at least 84dp with `maxLines = 1`, a weighted sparkline, and a figure column with `maxLines = 1` and no overflow handling ([ProfileSurfaceSections.kt:298-375](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L298-L375)). At 200%, "LEAN MASS" in mono `labelLarge` needs about 150dp of a 312dp measure. The sparkline collapses, the figure is clipped, and "Measure your first" shrinks to an ellipsis. Coach, warmup, the swap picker and the six recipes have goldens; Profile has none. `ProfileBodyRows` builds two `hiltViewModel`s internally ([ProfileSurfaceSections.kt:245-256](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileSurfaceSections.kt#L245-L256)), so a test can't render it as written.
- **Why it matters:** Users on large fonts lose the figure, or the row's only verb, on the rows they tap to log. Every drift in this report reached the tree without a failing check.
- **Proposal:** When `fontScale > 1.3`, lay the row out on lines: label and pill on the first, figure and delta on the second, and the sparkline full width beneath. This builds on PRO-02's rails. Split `ProfileBodyRows` into a stateful wrapper and a content composable that takes the metric list, the way `SwapPickerContent` is split. Then add a `ProfileScreenshotTest` that pins `ProfileAllTime`, `ProfileActivityMonth` and the body-rows content at 100%, 200%, AMOLED, monochrome and zero. Revised: the content split and the layout tie-in were added.
- **Doctrine:** This fixes FAILURES "Silent scale break". §14 asks for every touched screen to be checked at 100% and 200%. It follows the existing golden practice. DECISIONS 2026-07-27 notes that goldens catch layout rather than small content, which is exactly the class of drift here.

### PRO-08 · The bodyweight goal is loaded but deliberately not drawn, so the WEIGHT trend has no target
feature-gap · impact low · effort S · Profile → BODY → WEIGHT row

The screen collects it and then discards it with `@Suppress("UNUSED_EXPRESSION")`, beside a comment calling this "a real loss this direction causes" ([ProfileScreen.kt:158](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileScreen.kt#L158), [:472-475](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileScreen.kt#L472-L475)). - **Why it matters:** Someone cutting to 78 kg sees their weight trend on the page that owns bodyweight with no sense of how far away the target is.
- **Proposal:** Give `SurfaceSparkline` an optional `reference: Double?`, drawn dashed on the data-line rung, with the y-range widened to include it. On the WEIGHT row, pass the goal converted to the display unit, and add "goal 78 kg" to the spoken reading. Check it on a device with a realistic 3 to 5 kg gap. If the 24dp trend flattens beyond reading, delete the subscription and MAP's line instead. Either path closes the P4. Revised: impact lowered, because the goal is already readable on Goals and on Home's GOALS line.
- **Doctrine:** §1 counts a threshold line as data. It is a different mark from the Goals meter, so it doesn't echo it (§4.3). Nothing in SETTLED removed it. It was traded away in code without a record.

### PRO-10 · ACTIVITY knows the plan but never says how many planned training days were hit
feature-gap · impact low · effort S · Profile → ACTIVITY readings line

- **Now:** Each cell already resolves `planOn(date)` ([ProfileActivityMonth.kt:290-291](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L290-L291)). A planned training day with no session is read aloud as "no session on a training day" ([ProfileActivityMonth.kt:424](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L424)) but drawn as a plain empty square. The readings are ACTIVE DAYS and SESSIONS only ([ProfileActivityMonth.kt:357-358](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L357-L358)). Neither Stats' adherence heatmap nor Home reports planned-day hits.
- **Why it matters:** On a fixed four-day plan, the month question is "how many of my planned days did I hit". Today the answer means counting empty squares against lit ones by eye.
- **Proposal:** When any elapsed day of the shown month has `planOn == false`, replace ACTIVE DAYS with "11 OF 13", labelled PLANNED DAYS. The numerator is planned training days with a gym session. The denominator is planned training days up to today, or the whole month for a past month, minus vacation days (the same bridge the streak uses, [StatsRepository.kt:287-291](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsRepository.kt#L287-L291)). Fall back to ACTIVE DAYS in sequence mode or before the schedule was recorded. Build it on PRO-05's gym count. Revised: vacation days are excluded, and impact is lowered because the grid already shows misses.
- **Doctrine:** §4.9: show the reading. §12: an n-of-m figure uses the real denominator. It counts days against days, the unit the mark draws (FAILURES "Two units, one section"). It goes on an existing line, not a new section (§2①).

### PRO-14 · ACTIVITY and the ALL TIME week tallies ignore the "Week starts" setting
consistency · impact low · effort S · Profile → ACTIVITY grid and weekday header; ALL TIME "N this week · M last"

- **Now:** Settings → Format → Week starts (`firstDayMonday`, [ForgeUiSettings.kt:27](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/theme/ForgeUiSettings.kt#L27)) controls Home's week strip ([OverviewScreen.kt:396](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L396), [:624](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L624)), Stats' weekly figures ([StatsRepository.kt:127-143](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsRepository.kt#L127-L143)), the widget and the Gallery's "This week" ([MirrorTestControls.kt:94-102](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/MirrorTestControls.kt#L94-L102)). ACTIVITY hard-codes Monday as the first column ([ProfileActivityMonth.kt:185-188](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L185-L188), [:447-460](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L447-L460)). The ALL TIME tallies use ISO weeks ([ProfileRepository.kt:149-159](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/ProfileRepository.kt#L149-L159), [:296-297](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/ProfileRepository.kt#L296-L297)).
- **Why it matters:** A user whose week starts on Sunday sees Sun–Sat on Home and Mon–Sun one tap away. On a Sunday, Home's new week has begun while Profile's "3 this week" still counts the old one.
- **Proposal:** Read `LocalForgeSettings.current.firstDayMonday` in `ProfileActivityMonth` for the grid start and the header order. Planned rest is resolved per date, so it is unaffected. Pass the setting into `ProfileRepository.load` and key the tallies with `userWeekStart` ([WeekMath.kt:27](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/core/time/WeekMath.kt#L27)) instead of `IsoFields`. Stats' `CalendarHeatmap` makes the same Monday-first assumption; pass that to the Stats owner.
- **Doctrine:** SETTLED 2026-09-26 kept Week starts because it is wired ([SETTLED.md:36-37](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L36-L37)). A setting that some screens ignore drifts back toward "a control that changes nothing". The only visual change is column order.
