# Cardio tab

Scope: the Cardio tab, the weeks page, the week page, session detail and the log sheet (`ui/cardio/` with `components/` and `state/`), plus the Home and hub wiring into them and the domain they read (`domain/cardio`, `domain/engine`). 16 raised, 13 kept (9 revised), 3 dropped. Two parts cut from kept findings are listed with the dropped ones.

The 2026-08-23 rebuild holds up. The hero, the two lenses, the weeks chart and session detail all follow the doctrine. The debt is in where the numbers come from. The hero meter ignores the only weekly target a user can set now. Every speed reads as running pace, durations round to whole minutes, and an activity with no distance never gets a comparison. The one input the engine reads to price cardio against lifting sits behind "More", and Home's "Log cardio" stops a tap short of the form. The rest is edge polish: figure rows that ellipsize at 200%, one session drawn three ways, and a few marks that don't match their neighbours.

## Findings

### CAR-16 · Session-detail steps show the whole day without marking the session, and the tab's steps header doesn't say "today"

### CAR-09 · The weeks chart shows minutes only, so weekly distance can't be compared
feature-gap · impact low · effort M · Weeks page (`CardioWeeksScreen`)

- **Now:** `CardioWeekPoint` already carries `distanceKm` for every week ([CardioWeekSeries.kt:19](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/cardio/CardioWeekSeries.kt#L19)). `CardioWeekBars` draws minutes only ([CardioWeekBars.kt:44](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/cardio/components/CardioWeekBars.kt#L44)), the dashed rule is minutes, and both hero figures are minutes. A past week's distance appears only on that week's own page, one week at a time.
- **Why it matters:** Anyone watching weekly mileage has to open weeks one by one and remember the numbers. It matters to the runners among the users, not to most lifters, which is why this is graded low.
- **Proposal:** Above the chart, show a `ForgeSlidingSegments` "Minutes · Distance", only when some entry has a distance. In Distance mode the bars read `distanceKm`. The rule and "cleared" read a weekly CARDIO_DISTANCE goal if one exists. With none, there's no rule and the hero drops to its one average figure, since WHO has no distance reference. When more than one activity has logged distance, reuse the pace trend's activity selector so a 40 km ride and a 10 km run aren't summed. Revised: the no-goal state is defined, and impact is downgraded from medium.
- **Doctrine:** §2③ switching the view is a segment, never a button. SETTLED 2026-08-23: one bar per week, tap a bar to open it, no week pager, no LOAD chart on the tab ([SETTLED.md:222-233](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L222-L233)). All of that is unchanged. FAILURES *Two units, one section*: the hero figures switch with the bars.
