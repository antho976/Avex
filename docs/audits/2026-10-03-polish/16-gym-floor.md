# On the gym floor (journey)

Scope: one workout followed on the floor: the live session (`ui/gym/train/**`), the session and rest-timer notifications (`service/WorkoutSessionService.kt` and its bridge), the wrist bridge (`service/wear/`), the widget and the watch app with its glances, compared with freestyle (`ui/gym/freestyle/`). 18 raised, 7 kept (7 revised), 11 dropped.

The logging core holds up. Most of what this finder saw on the session screen is already raised elsewhere in this pass: 01-train-flow, 02-train-logging, 05-home-nav, 13-academy-wear and 15-first-week together cover eleven of the eighteen findings. The table at the end points to those reports instead of repeating them. What's left is what only this journey sees: the moments the lifter isn't looking at the session screen. With the phone locked, the notification shows a minute counter and nothing else. After the phone jumps to another exercise, the watch keeps logging the old one. Two common ways to train, adding load to a bodyweight lift and alternating two exercises, are stored in the data model but can't be entered. On the screen itself the remaining debt is small: two accent actions and two rest clocks, where there should be one of each. Every change to `GYM_DAY` needs Antho's sign-off ([SETTLED.md:138](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L138)).

## Findings

### GYM-01 · A locked phone shows no rest countdown, no next set and no controls
feature-gap · impact high · effort M · Session notification and rest-done alert

- **Now:** The ongoing notification shows the day name over "23 min" ([WorkoutSessionService.kt:154](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionService.kt#L154)). A 60-second loop rebuilds it ([WorkoutSessionService.kt:140](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionService.kt#L140)) from a `SessionNotifState` that carries only `dayName` and `startedAtMs` ([WorkoutSessionBridge.kt:46](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionBridge.kt#L46)). It has no chronometer and no actions: `setUsesChronometer`, `setChronometerCountDown` and `addAction` appear nowhere in the app or wear modules. When a rest ends, the heads-up reads "Rest timer done / Time to get back to it" on every set ([WorkoutSessionService.kt:191-192](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionService.kt#L191-L192)). +30 s and Skip exist only on the session screen and on the watch, and the watch reaches them through `SessionTimerHolder.controller` ([WearSyncService.kt:82-83](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WearSyncService.kt#L82-L83), [SessionTimerHolder.kt:41](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/SessionTimerHolder.kt#L41)). The data a richer notification needs is already assembled. `RestTimerController.state` holds the countdown. `WatchSessionMirror.sessionLive` holds the exercise name and "set n of m" ([WatchSessionMirror.kt:152-165](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WatchSessionMirror.kt#L152-L165)).
- **Why it matters:** A lifter without a watch who locks the phone between sets, or has keep-screen-on turned off, has to unlock it to see how much rest is left, add 30 s, or see what comes next. The rest-done alert is the one notification such a user sees on every set, and it says nothing about the set.
- **Proposal:**
  1. The service injects `SessionTimerHolder` and `WatchSessionMirror` and collects `controller.state` and `sessionLive`. It rebuilds the ongoing notification only on structural changes (start, pause, ±30, stop), never on every tick.
     - **Rest running:** `setUsesChronometer(true)`, `setChronometerCountDown(true)`, `setWhen(endAtMs)`, with the content "Bench Press · set 3 of 4".
     - **Rest paused:** "Rest paused · 1:12 left", with no chronometer.
     - **Between rests:** the exercise line plus elapsed minutes.
  2. While a rest runs, add two actions, "+30s" and "Skip". They go through one small non-exported `BroadcastReceiver` that calls `controller.addSeconds(30)` and `controller.stop()`, the same calls the wrist makes.
  3. Rewrite the done alert from the same data: "Rest done" over "Next: Bench Press · set 3 of 4".
  4. Call `setVisibility(VISIBILITY_PUBLIC)` on the session notification so a secure lock screen shows its content.

  Add weight × reps to the line only after step 1 of ACW-01 ([13-academy-wear.md](13-academy-wear.md)) lands. Until then the mirror's target weight is this session's highest-index set, usually a back-off set ([WatchSessionMirror.kt:127](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WatchSessionMirror.kt#L127)). RCH-02 ([14-reachability.md](14-reachability.md)) adds `dayKey` to `SessionNotifState` for the tap destination, so land the two together. Revised from the original in three ways: the service reads the timer from the controller instead of threading it through `SessionNotifState`, the weight text waits for ACW-01, and the notification is made visible on the lock screen.
- **Doctrine:** §4.6 bans in-page banners and strips. This is the OS notification that already exists, with no new channel and nothing drawn in the app. §11 says a generated notification line states a fact from the user's data and varies with it, and the fixed "Time to get back to it" fails that. The WEAR.md haptic handoff is untouched, because `handleTimerDone` keeps its ack grace period and its quiet-hours branch. It works offline: only a local notification and a receiver. No SETTLED entry covers notification content, and the live screen doesn't change.

### GYM-12 · The watch keeps logging the exercise the phone moved away from
ux-friction · impact medium · effort M · Watch set view and the phone's Up Next jump

- **Now:** The exercise in focus on the phone is held in a Compose `remember` ([DaySessionContent.kt:149](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/DaySessionContent.kt#L149)), which Up Next and DONE taps set ([DaySessionContent.kt:362](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/DaySessionContent.kt#L362)). The watch's current slot comes from `CurrentSlotResolver` instead: the last logged slot while it is short of plan, otherwise the next incomplete slot after it ([CurrentSlotResolver.kt:22-42](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/CurrentSlotResolver.kt#L22-L42)). That one rule decides both what the watch shows ([WatchSessionMirror.kt:103](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WatchSessionMirror.kt#L103)) and where a wrist log is written ([SetLogUseCase.kt:100](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/session/SetLogUseCase.kt#L100)). `WearFocusHolder` carries only early-done marks ([WearFocusHolder.kt:19-46](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WearFocusHolder.kt#L19-L46)), and the watch has no control for changing exercise. Say the pulldown station is taken and the lifter jumps to rows on the phone. The watch keeps showing pulldowns, and a tap of Log set writes a pulldown set.
- **Why it matters:** This hits watch users who train out of plan order, which is the normal response to a busy gym. They either take the phone out to log, which defeats the point of the watch, or log a set against the wrong lift, which corrupts the history of both lifts.
- **Proposal:** Move the shown exercise into `DayUiState` and set it through a `DayUiEvent.FocusExercise(id)` from every place that changes it today: Up Next, DONE rows, MOVE TO NEXT and skip. The ViewModel also writes `WearFocusHolder.focus(sessionId, slotId, atMs)`. `CurrentSlotResolver.resolve` takes the focused slot and the time focus moved to it. If that move is newer than the last logged set and the slot is incomplete, the focused slot wins; otherwise today's rule applies. The mirror adds the focus flow to the `combine` it already runs over `earlyDone`. Revised from the original, which said both "prefer the focused slot" and "a set on any other slot still wins"; whichever happened more recently now decides. Holding focus in the ViewModel also closes two P3s from the 2026-09-26 audit: rotation loses the focused exercise, and "Apply →" leaves the old first exercise in focus ([01-gym-train.md](../2026-09-26/01-gym-train.md)).
- **Doctrine:** WEAR.md: the watch answers "what now", one decision per screen. This adds no wrist control and no protocol field, because `SessionLiveDto` already carries the exercise. The live screen's layout doesn't change. ACW-02's end-of-day state ([13-academy-wear.md](13-academy-wear.md)) is a separate fix and works alongside this one.

### GYM-11 · Bodyweight lifts can't record added weight or assistance
feature-gap · impact medium · effort M · Live session and freestyle: Pull-Up, Chin-Up, Dip and other BODYWEIGHT slots

- **Now:** On a BODYWEIGHT slot the weight cell is the fixed text "BW" ([SetInputRow.kt:374-378](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L374-L378)), and every set logs as "BW" ([SetInputRow.kt:247-248](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L247-L248)). Freestyle hides the weight field for bodyweight moves the same way ([FreestyleLogParts.kt:479](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/freestyle/FreestyleLogParts.kt#L479)). `LoggedSet.isAssisted` exists ([LoggedSet.kt:56](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/db/entities/LoggedSet.kt#L56)), and PR reads already skip assisted sets ([WatchSessionMirror.kt:142](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WatchSessionMirror.kt#L142)). Its only writer, `ToggleAssisted`, is never dispatched ([DayExerciseHandlers.kt:201-205](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/DayExerciseHandlers.kt#L201-L205)). `WeightParser` reads "BW" as no weight at all ([WeightParser.kt:35](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/parser/WeightParser.kt#L35)). The library has no weighted or assisted variant of Pull-Up, Chin-Up or Dip to swap to ([ExerciseLibrary.kt:142-153](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/program/ExerciseLibrary.kt#L142-L153), [:598](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/program/ExerciseLibrary.kt#L598)).
- **Why it matters:** Someone doing pull-ups with a 10 kg belt, or with 30 kg of band or machine help, can't record the number that is actually changing. Their history reads as a flat "BW × 8", and the coach can't see their progress on those lifts.
- **Proposal:** On BODYWEIGHT slots, keep "BW" as the big figure and add one `StepperPill` beside REPS for load. It reads "+10 KG" above zero and "−20 KG" below zero, labelled ADDED or ASSIST. Store added load as `weightText = "BW+10"` with `weightLb` set to the added load, and assistance as `"BW-20"` with `isAssisted = true` and `weightLb = null`. Teach `WeightParser` the "BW±n" form, and render it through `WeightFormatter` in `SetRow`, the plan line and freestyle's entry slab. Two things need settling first:
  - Volume counts only the added load, because the set doesn't know the lifter's bodyweight.
  - BUG_SCAN's area-1 HIGH ([BUG_SCAN.md:305](../../../BUG_SCAN.md#L305)) must be fixed before anything writes `isAssisted`: assisted sets are kept out of PRs but still count in PB display, goals and trophies.

  Revised from the original: it names the volume rule and the prerequisite, and it adds freestyle so the two loggers behave alike. TRL-06 ([02-train-logging.md](02-train-logging.md)) covers rep-based bodyweight PRs and stays a separate item.
- **Doctrine:** §13: hot-path numbers use steppers, never keyboard-first entry. §11: weights go through `WeightFormatter` in the user's unit, never a hardcoded "kg". The only new chrome is one stepper in an existing row, which fits the Live archetype. No SETTLED entry or plan covers this. It edits the untouchable screen, so it needs sign-off.

### GYM-10 · Supersets are stored, imported and respected by the engine, but can't be run
feature-gap · impact medium · effort L · Live session Up Next, rest timer, wrist slot resolver

- **Now:** `LoggedExercise.supersetGroup` exists ([LoggedExercise.kt:51-55](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/db/entities/LoggedExercise.kt#L51-L55)). It round-trips through the JSON importer ([ForgeJsonImporter.kt:155](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/importer/ForgeJsonImporter.kt#L155)) and is read from Hevy's `superset_id` column ([HevyImporter.kt:8](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/importer/HevyImporter.kt#L8)). `OrderingAdvisor` keeps each group together ([OrderingAdvisor.kt:43-47](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/adapt/OrderingAdvisor.kt#L43-L47)). The only writer, `SetSupersetGroup`, is never dispatched, and it gives up if the partner exercise has no logged row yet ([DayExerciseHandlers.kt:242-247](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/DayExerciseHandlers.kt#L242-L247)). To alternate A and B today, the lifter opens Up Next and taps the other exercise, twice per round ([ExerciseCardComponents.kt:596-601](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/ExerciseCardComponents.kt#L596-L601)). Every LOG SET also starts the full rest ([DayExerciseHandlers.kt:353](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/DayExerciseHandlers.kt#L353)), even when B comes next.
- **Why it matters:** Pairing accessories is the usual way to save time. Each round costs four extra taps and a rest timer to dismiss. Someone moving from Hevy finds their supersets imported but can't repeat them.
- **Proposal:**
  - **Pairing:** In the expanded Up Next group, add one whole-row member, "Superset with Lateral Raise", for the next incomplete exercise. It runs `ensureLoggedExercise` on both slots, then `setSupersetGroup`.
  - **While a pair is live:** the Up Next header reads "SUPERSET · LATERAL RAISE", and the same row reads "Unpair".
  - **Logging:** Logging A while B has sets left moves focus to B and starts no rest. Logging B starts A's prescribed rest and returns focus to A.
  - **Watch:** `SetLogUseCase` follows the same rule, and `CurrentSlotResolver` treats a group as one unit, so the watch alternates too.

  Revised from the original, whose entry point was the long-press menu that TRF-14 ([01-train-flow.md](01-train-flow.md)) proposes deleting. This depends on GYM-12, which moves focus into the ViewModel.
- **Doctrine:** Nothing in SETTLED removes supersets, and FEATURES.md never listed them. The Up Next group already holds one row per upcoming exercise plus "+ add exercise", so this adds one more row, not new chrome (§2③, the whole row is the tap target). §9: haptics stay at set-log only. The screen is untouchable, so it needs the owner's sign-off. Effort is L because rest, focus and the watch all change.

### GYM-16 · The rest countdown is drawn twice, and the floating copy clips at large text sizes
ui-polish · impact low · effort S · Live session inline rest row and floating rest bubble

- **Now:** While a timer runs, the card in focus draws an inline "REST 1:24 / 2:00 · done" row under the last set ([ExerciseCard.kt:344-345](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/ExerciseCard.kt#L344-L345), [ExerciseCardComponents.kt:411-456](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/ExerciseCardComponents.kt#L411-L456)). At the same time, the Scaffold's floating-button slot draws a ringed bubble with the same countdown ([DayScreen.kt:145-163](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/DayScreen.kt#L145-L163)). Tapping either one opens the timer controls. "done" exists only on the inline row, and hold-for-+30 s only on the bubble ([DayScreen.kt:159](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/DayScreen.kt#L159)). The bubble is a fixed `.size(64.dp)` disc ([RestTimerBubble.kt:148](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/RestTimerBubble.kt#L148)) holding a `titleMedium` figure over an 8sp label ([RestTimerBubble.kt:239-249](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/RestTimerBubble.kt#L239-L249)). At 200% that becomes a 32sp figure plus a 16sp label in a 64dp disc that can't grow. No screenshot test covers the bubble, and the `live-200` golden shows the recipe, not this screen.
- **Why it matters:** Between sets, this is the screen the lifter reads most, and it shows one fact on two clocks. Each clock has its own shortcut, and neither points to the other. At 200%, the countdown is clipped in the bubble, the copy that stays in place when the lifter scrolls.
- **Proposal:** Hide the bubble while the inline row is on screen. Track that with `onGloballyPositioned` against the list's viewport, so the bubble appears when the row scrolls away or the keyboard covers it. Give the inline row the bubble's long-press +30 s. Size the bubble with `sizeIn(minWidth = 64.dp, minHeight = 64.dp)`, and cap its figure's font scale at about 1.3×, as the serif hero does. TRL-08 recolours the same inline row, so coordinate with it. Revised from the original in two ways: the bubble's sizing fix moved here from GYM-15, and visibility is measured on the row itself rather than the list item, because the card item is taller than the screen.
- **Doctrine:** §4.3 "one home" and FAILURES "Mark echo": a fact appears once per screen. §14 bans a fixed size on any container that holds text, and allows clamping a hero. Both clocks stay, and each shows only when it's the one in view. The screen is untouchable, so it needs sign-off.
