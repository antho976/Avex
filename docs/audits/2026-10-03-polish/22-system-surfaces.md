# What leaves the app: notification channels and content, permission path, PDF and share outputs, widget picker, inbound share

Scope: OS notifications and their channels (`service/ForgeNotifications`, `WeeklyRecapWorker`, `TrainingReminderWorker`, `WorkoutSessionService`), the notification-permission row and Settings → Notifications, the session PDF, Quick export and the session JSON share, the share/open-with import confirm, the widget provider metadata, the app-icon caption and the Health Connect rationale entry (`MainActivity.kt`, `AndroidManifest.xml`, `res/xml/forge_widget_info.xml`, `res/layout/widget_initial_layout.xml`). 13 raised, 12 kept (8 revised), 1 dropped.

Inside the app this layer is careful: the feed, the blocked-state notice, export error handling and the lock gating all hold. The debt is in what crosses the boundary. On Android 13+ nothing ever asks for the notification grant, so every push depends on the user finding a setup row and an OS page. The pushes that do arrive are fixed one-liners that misread cardio weeks, stay in the shade after the app has dealt with them, and can mostly be muted only from Android. Files going out (the session PDF, the CSVs, the JSON share) and the file coming in (the share-to-import confirm) read like plumbing: a one-page cap, raw codes, header-only files, a confirm that names nothing.

## Findings

### SYS-01 · Android 13+ never asks for the notification grant, so the recap, the coach push and rest alerts stay off until the user finds a setup row and an OS page
ux-friction · impact high · effort S · First run on Android 13+; the feed's "Turn on notifications" row; Settings → Notifications

- **Now:** `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` is declared ([AndroidManifest.xml:6](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml#L6)) and checked ([NotificationFeed.kt:461-466](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L461-L466)), but nothing in the phone module requests it; the comment at [MainActivity.kt:431-434](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L431-L434) says so. The watch asks for its own grant ([wear MainActivity.kt:46-48](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/MainActivity.kt#L46-L48)). With targetSdk 36 the phone grant starts denied, and creating channels doesn't prompt for an app that targets 13+. The weekly recap and rest-timer alerts default to on ([SettingsRepository.kt:242-243](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/prefs/SettingsRepository.kt#L242-L243)), but none of them can post; the rest timer only vibrates ([WorkoutSessionService.kt:180-199](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionService.kt#L180-L199)). The way back is the feed's SETUP row "Turn on notifications" ([NotificationFeed.kt:272-285](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L272-L285)). It counts toward the bell's badge ([ForgeNavHost.kt:208](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L208)) but sorts last ([NotificationFeed.kt:304](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L304)). A tap opens `ACTION_APP_NOTIFICATION_SETTINGS` ([NotificationsScreen.kt:96-101](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/notifications/NotificationsScreen.kt#L96-L101)), so a user who was never asked has to go bell, row, OS page, toggle, Back. Settings → Notifications opens with "Phone notifications are off for Avex" and every phone switch greyed out ([SettingsSubPages.kt:318-329](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/settings/SettingsSubPages.kt#L318-L329)), although the user never turned anything off.
- **Why it matters:** Most installs run Android 13+. Until the user acts, the Monday coach-brief push (the way into PRODUCT's Monday ritual), the recap and the rest-done alert never arrive. Users who do find the row pay four steps for what Android offers as one tap.
- **Proposal:** Add one helper in `ui/common`, `rememberEnableNotifications(): () -> Unit`.
  - On API 33+, when a new pref `NOTIF_PERM_REQUESTED` is false, it launches `ActivityResultContracts.RequestPermission(POST_NOTIFICATIONS)`, sets the pref, and calls `NotificationFeed.refresh()` on the result.
  - In every other case (API below 33, already requested, denied) it opens `ACTION_APP_NOTIFICATION_SETTINGS`, as today.
  - Don't reuse `NOTIF_PERM_ASKED`. That key means "the row was cleared" and hides the row ([NotificationFeed.kt:386-388](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L386-L388)).
  - Wire the helper to `NoticeAction.EnableNotifications` and to the Settings notice. While the grant has never been requested, the notice reads "Avex can't send phone notifications yet" with the action "Allow notifications". After a denial it keeps today's title and "Open system settings". Nothing runs at launch or during onboarding.

  Revised: the finder said nothing tells the user. The row is in the bell's badge from first launch, so it can be found; the problem is how slow it is. The new pref is kept apart from `NOTIF_PERM_ASKED`. This changes what a settled row does, so it needs Antho's sign-off.
- **Doctrine:** SETTLED 2026-07-27 removed the cold-launch rationale dialog ([SETTLED.md:89-92](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L89-L92)). This adds no dialog of Avex's own and nothing at launch: the OS sheet appears only after a tap on a row whose one job is turning notifications on. Only the very first tap changes, and it is not a re-request; every later tap still opens the OS screen. MAP's kit rule ("A denied permission disables the rows it silences", [MAP.md:579](../../../.claude/design/MAP.md#L579)) is unchanged. ONB-16's "no prompt from onboarding" ([12-onboarding-builder.md](12-onboarding-builder.md)) holds.

### SYS-02 · A week of only cardio gets the come-back nudge and no recap
missing-state · impact medium · effort S · Monday noon push (weekly recap or come-back nudge)

- **Now:** `lastCompletedWeekStats()` returns gym sessions as `workouts` and cardio only as `cardioMinutes` ([StatsRepository.kt:311-330](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsRepository.kt#L311-L330)). The worker branches on `stats.workouts == 0` alone ([WeeklyRecapWorker.kt:113-131](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L113-L131)). It posts "Ready when you are" / "No pressure, your plan's right where you left it…" and returns, so the recap and its cardio clause ([WeeklyRecapWorker.kt:142](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L142)) never run. A week of runs, a travel week of runs, or a planned week off the bar with 150 minutes of cardio all get the lapse message. No prior audit tracks this.
- **Why it matters:** Someone who trained all week, just not under a bar, is told they lapsed, and the work they did goes unrecapped. The nudge lands on exactly the users who were active.
- **Proposal:** Add `cardioSessions` to `CompletedWeekStats`; the rows are already loaded by `cardioDao.between(fromMs, toMs)`, which excludes rest entries. The week counts as active when `workouts > 0 || cardioSessions > 0`, and the come-back nudge fires only when both are zero. A cardio-only week gets a recap built from cardio, for example "No lifting logged · 3 cardio sessions, 150 min". Same channel, same `weeklyRecapEnabled` gate. Land it with SYS-04, which rebuilds the same body.
- **Doctrine:** PRODUCT voice (binding): generated lines are grounded in the user's numbers ([PRODUCT.md:98-102](../../../PRODUCT.md#L98-L102)). §11 rules out claims the data doesn't support, and this removes blame the data doesn't support ([DESIGN.md:266](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L266)). No channel, notice kind or surface is added. RCH-10 rewrites the come-back copy ([14-reachability.md](14-reachability.md)); this changes only when it fires, so the two compose.

### SYS-03 · Pushes stay in the shade after the app has dealt with what they announce
ux-friction · impact medium · effort S · Notification shade: coach brief, deload, training reminder, come-back nudge, "Rest timer done"

- **Now:** Nothing in the phone module cancels a posted notification; there is no `cancel(` on a notification id and no `setTimeoutAfter`. "Your coach has an update" (2003, [WeeklyRecapWorker.kt:86](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L86)) survives the user opening the brief from the launcher, even though `CoachRepository.markSeen` clears the feed row ([CoachRepository.kt:1095](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/CoachRepository.kt#L1095)). The daily reminder (2002, [TrainingReminderWorker.kt:90](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/TrainingReminderWorker.kt#L90)) says "today" and is still there after that day's session is finished. The come-back nudge (2004, [WeeklyRecapWorker.kt:124](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L124)) outlives the next logged session. "Rest timer done" ([WorkoutSessionService.kt:199](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionService.kt#L199)) outlives every later set and the workout itself, because `onDestroy` only cancels the coroutine scope ([WorkoutSessionService.kt:240-243](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionService.kt#L240-L243)). Only a tap on the notification removes it (`setAutoCancel`).
- **Why it matters:** The shade disagrees with the app: an "update" already read, a "train today" for a workout already done, a rest alert from an hour ago. Stale pushes teach people to swipe Avex away unread, and that costs the one push that matters, the Monday brief.
- **Proposal:**
  - Move the notification ids into `ForgeNotifications` (it already documents the 200x band, [ForgeNotifications.kt:73](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/ForgeNotifications.kt#L73)) and add `clear(context, vararg ids)` over `NotificationManagerCompat`.
  - Call it from `CoachRepository.markSeen` for the brief and deload pushes. That is the one point the feed, the Coach tab and Clear all share ([CoachViewModel.kt:189](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachViewModel.kt#L189), [NotificationFeed.kt:354](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L354), [:417](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L417)).
  - Call it for the reminder and the come-back nudge from a new `finishSideEffect` in `WorkoutRepository.finishAfterCommit` ([WorkoutRepository.kt:281-308](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/WorkoutRepository.kt#L281-L308)) and from `CardioRepository.add` ([CardioRepository.kt:36-40](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/CardioRepository.kt#L36-L40)).
  - Give the reminder `setTimeoutAfter` until local midnight, since its text says "today".
  - Give the rest-done alert a short `setTimeoutAfter` (about two minutes), and clear it in `onDestroy`. Once GYM-01's controller collector lands ([16-gym-floor.md](16-gym-floor.md)), clear it when the next rest starts instead.

  Revised: the service has no "rest started" event today, so the rest-alert clear is a timeout until GYM-01 gives it one.
- **Doctrine:** Behaviour only: no channel, row or copy is added, and nothing is drawn in the app (§4.6). It extends DECISIONS 2026-07-27's "acting on a row clears it" to the OS copy of the same notice. GYM-01 rewrites the rest-done alert's content and RCH-02 its tap target; neither clears it, so this composes with both.

### SYS-04 · The weekly recap is one run-on line, with a per-set volume format, no comparison, and the "On this day" memory last and stripped of its numbers
ui-polish · impact medium · effort S · Weekly recap push (Monday noon)

- **Now:** The body is a `buildString` of readings joined by " · " ([WeeklyRecapWorker.kt:139-160](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L139-L160)): workouts, volume, cardio, streak, then "On this day (3mo ago): Push A". The title is a fixed "Weekly recap" that names no week ([WeeklyRecapWorker.kt:162](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L162)). Volume goes through `formatWeight` ([WeeklyRecapWorker.kt:141](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L141)), the per-set path, so a week prints "24350 lb", "11045.2 kg" or, in stones, "1739 st 4 lb"; `formatVolumeCompact` exists for aggregates ([WeightFormatter.kt:101](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/units/WeightFormatter.kt#L101)). The memory carries `totalVolumeLb` and `prCount` ([StatsRepository.kt:612-618](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsRepository.kt#L612-L618)), but only `dayName` is printed, as the last clause, so the collapsed notification cuts it. Nothing compares the week with the one before, although the same prior-week `aggregateInRange` read already feeds `buildWeekComparison` ([StatsRepository.kt:623-637](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsRepository.kt#L623-L637)). FEATURES lists "On this day" memories under Profile ([FEATURES.md:85](../../../.claude/FEATURES.md#L85)), and the recap is now the feature's only home.
- **Why it matters:** The app's one weekly retrospective reads like a log line. The parts worth reading (up or down on last week, what you did three months ago) are missing or cut off, and stones users get an aggregate that makes no sense.
- **Proposal:** Keep one notification, laid out in lines through the existing `BigTextStyle`.

  - Line 1, which is also the collapsed text: "12.3k kg lifted, 1.1k more than the week before", through `formatVolumeCompact` and the prior-week aggregate.
  - Line 2, only when present: "45 min cardio · 12-day streak".
  - Line 3: "3 months ago today: Push A, 14.2k kg, 2 PRs", from the fields the memory already carries.
  - Move FEATURES.md:85 to Reminders as "Weekly recap with an on-this-day memory".
- **Doctrine:** SETTLED keeps the memory in the weekly recap, "where a retrospective belongs" ([SETTLED.md:55-60](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L55-L60), [:98-99](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L98-L99)). This gives it a full line inside the recap and adds no in-app surface. §11: aggregates stay one decimal in every unit and k-abbreviate ([DESIGN.md:290-294](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L290-L294)). PRODUCT voice: lines vary with the numbers. Same channel, toggle and schedule. HOM-01's reason for not sending this push to Recap ([05-home-nav.md](05-home-nav.md)) is untouched.

### SYS-05 · Settings lists 3 of the 7 phone pushes, and Android shows only 2 of Avex's channels until each push has fired once
consistency · impact medium · effort M · Settings → Notifications "On your phone"; Android's App info → Notifications

- **Now:** The app posts seven kinds of push; "On your phone" has switches for three: Training reminders, Weekly recap and Rest timer alerts ([SettingsSubPages.kt:331-364](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/settings/SettingsSubPages.kt#L331-L364)).   - Add two `SettingsSwitchRow`s to "On your phone": "Coach briefs" (shown while the coach is active) and "Come-back nudge", each gating its worker branch.
  - When `getNotificationChannel(id).importance == IMPORTANCE_NONE`, that row renders disabled with "Off in Android settings", and a tap opens `ACTION_CHANNEL_NOTIFICATION_SETTINGS` for the channel.

  Revised: the finder folded the deload channel into Coach briefs, which would break RIT-13's fallback; it stays as its own channel.
- **Doctrine:** DECISIONS 2026-07-27 split the page by where a thing arrives and refused group captions ([DECISIONS.md:318-337](../../../.claude/design/DECISIONS.md#L318-L337)). The new rows join "On your phone" with no caption, and the quiet-hours line "Mutes the phone alerts above" ([SettingsSubPages.kt:369](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/settings/SettingsSubPages.kt#L369)) becomes true as written.
