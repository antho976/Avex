# Reachability and wayfinding (cross-app)

Scope: the hub and its routes (`ui/nav/Routes.kt`, `ForgeNavHost.kt`, `HubScreen.kt`, `ForgeBottomBar.kt`), every `navigate`/`onOpen*` callback under `ui/`, `service/ForgeNotifications.kt` and the services that post through it, `MainActivity`'s intent handling, and the `widget/` package. 14 raised, 7 kept (7 revised), 7 dropped.

Inside the app the hub is sound: five swipe tabs, Back returns to Home, single-top pushes, and the widget's event pipeline (M-30) waits for both the program load and the app lock. The debt is at the edges, where Android hands the user in. Every push and both session notifications open `MainActivity` with no destination, so a warm tap resumes whatever screen was last open. The widget has the app's only deep-link path, and no notification uses it. Inside the app, a few doors lead to the wrong place: Home's goal rows, a go-Home hold that only exists on Home, and an import receipt with nowhere to go. The biggest gaps this finder raised (choosing a day, Recap, per-lift history, note search, Home's cardio rows, the cardio CTA, the rest-day widget) are already covered by sibling reports in this pass. The table at the end points to them instead of repeating them.

## Findings

### RCH-02 · Pushes and session notifications open wherever the app was left, including the Monday coach brief
ux-friction · impact medium · effort M · OS notifications (coach brief, deload, reminder, come-back, weekly recap, session in progress, rest timer) and widget taps

- **Now:** `ForgeNotifications.build` gives every push the same content intent: `MainActivity`, `FLAG_ACTIVITY_SINGLE_TOP`, no extra ([ForgeNotifications.kt:47-53](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/ForgeNotifications.kt#L47-L53)). Its KDoc says the tap "lands on Overview", but only a cold tap does. That covers the coach brief ("Your coach has an update", [WeeklyRecapWorker.kt:86](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L86)), the deload push ([:100](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L100)), the come-back nudge ([:124](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L124)), the weekly recap ([:164](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L164)) and the daily reminder ([TrainingReminderWorker.kt:89](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/TrainingReminderWorker.kt#L89)). The in-progress and rest-timer notifications build the same bare intent ([WorkoutSessionService.kt:162](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionService.kt#L162), [:182](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionService.kt#L182)). The activity is `singleTask` ([AndroidManifest.xml:109](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml#L109)), so a warm tap resumes the top screen, Settings or History for example. `MainActivity` reads exactly one extra, the widget's `EXTRA_START_DAY_KEY` ([MainActivity.kt:196](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L196), [:405](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L405)). It wraps that extra in a sequenced `WidgetOpenRequest` that survives recreation ([MainActivity.kt:121](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L121)), and `ForgeNavHost` routes it once the app is unlocked and the program has loaded ([ForgeNavHost.kt:137-160](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L137-L160)). Two gaps sit in that path. The widget's gym branch pushes the day without first popping to the hub ([ForgeNavHost.kt:147](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L147)), unlike its cardio branch ([:149](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L149)) and the feed's coach row ([:376](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L376)), so a day opened from the widget while Settings was up sits on top of Settings. And the widget's freestyle and no-plan states read "Tap to log a workout" and "Tap to build your plan" but carry no parameter ([ForgeWidget.kt:125-129](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/widget/ForgeWidget.kt#L125-L129), [:225-226](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/widget/ForgeWidget.kt#L225-L226)), so both open Home.
- **Why it matters:** The coach-brief push is the way into PRODUCT's Monday ritual. Tapping it lands on Home, or on whatever deep screen was last open, and reaching the brief takes Back once per level plus the Coach tab. "Time for a deload week" behaves the same. A lifter who backed out of a live workout to Home, and then taps "Rest timer done" from the shade, also lands on Home. The finder's other claim, that the brief's feed row stays unread, does not hold: opening the Coach page marks the week seen ([CoachViewModel.kt:189](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachViewModel.kt#L189)).
- **Proposal:** One destination extra and one router.
  - `ForgeNotifications.build(..., open: String? = null)` writes `EXTRA_OPEN`. The values are `"coach"` for the brief and deload pushes, `"home"` for the reminder, come-back nudge and weekly recap, `"day:<key>"` for the in-progress and timer notifications, and `"freestyle"` / `"build"` for the widget's two empty states. Request codes stay per channel, and `FLAG_UPDATE_CURRENT` already replaces the extra. The weekly recap goes to Home, not Recap; HOM-01 ruled that out because Recap has no week view.
  - `SessionNotifState` gains the `dayKey` ([WorkoutSessionBridge.kt:46](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionBridge.kt#L46)). Its one writer already knows it ([DayViewModelRefresh.kt:229](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/DayViewModelRefresh.kt#L229)). This changes the payload the bridge carries, not the live screen.
  - Generalise `WidgetOpenRequest(id, dayKey)` into `ExternalOpenRequest(id, target)`, set from either extra. It keeps the saved-state restore, the `LocalAppLockActive` wait and consume-on-route.
  - In `ForgeNavHost`, route every request through one helper:
    - If the target is the gym day already on top, do nothing (`navigateToGymDay`'s existing guard).
    - Otherwise `popBackStack(Routes.OVERVIEW, false)`, as `goHome` and the feed already do, then:
      - `coach` sets `pendingHubPage = COACH`. A hidden tab is consumed harmlessly ([HubScreen.kt:97-104](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/HubScreen.kt#L97-L104)), and the coach pushes only post while that tab exists.
      - `home` sets `pendingHubPage = HOME`.
      - `day:<key>` goes through `widgetRoutingFor` and then `navigateToGymDay`.
      - `freestyle` pushes `FREESTYLE_LOG`, and `build` pushes `programBuilder()`.
  - The widget's gym branch uses the same helper, which fixes the Settings-underneath case.
  - The reminder lands on Home, not in a session. Home's hero decides what today is.
- **Doctrine:** Only where an OS tap lands changes. Nothing is drawn on any page, so §4.6 holds (notices stay in the feed, no banners), and none of SETTLED's three removed launch dialogs comes back. The proposal reuses the M-30 event shape and the lock gate instead of building a second path. Popping to the hub first is the 2026-09-26 audit 06 P3 fix ("pop to Overview first, as `goHome` and the widget path do"). The REST/TRAIN hero rule (SETTLED 2026-08-16) is untouched, because the reminder hands off to Home rather than starting a day. The live session screen (SETTLED, untouchable) is not edited.

### RCH-07 · Home's goal rows open the whole list, and at zero goals "Pin a goal" opens an empty one
discoverability · impact low · effort S · Home GOALS, Cardio Progress GOALS

- **Now:** Every goal row on Home calls `onOpenGoals` ([OverviewScreen.kt:675](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L675)), the same list that `view all` opens ([:650](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L650)). Cardio's goal rows do the same ([CardioComponents.kt:233](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/cardio/CardioComponents.kt#L233)). On the Goals screen, each row opens its own editor ([ForgeNavHost.kt:440-441](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L440-L441)). With no goals at all, Home's prompt reads "Pin a goal" ([OverviewScreen.kt:661](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L661)) and opens a list reading "Nothing tracked yet" ([GoalsScreen.kt:171](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/goals/GoalsScreen.kt#L171)) whose only control is the Add capsule. There is nothing to pin. Home already falls back to the top three goals when none are pinned ([OverviewScreen.kt:277-283](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L277-L283)), so "pin" only ever appears when it can't apply.
- **Why it matters:** A tap on "Bench Press 100 / 120 kg" lands on a ladder where the user has to find Bench again before they can change it. A first goal costs an extra tap behind a verb that names the wrong act.
- **Proposal:** Carry the source id on `GoalLineData`. The lift `exerciseId` and the custom goal `id` are already read to build the pin keys ([OverviewScreen.kt:246](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L246), [:260](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L260)). Add `onOpenGoal(exerciseId: String?, customId: Long?)` to `OverviewScreen` and `CardioGoalsSection`, wired in `HubScreen` to `Routes.goalEditor(...)` exactly as the Goals screen does. `view all` keeps opening the list. At zero, the prompt reads "Set a goal" and opens `Routes.goalEditor()`. The editor's `popIfCurrent` exit ([ForgeNavHost.kt:456](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L456)) returns to Home. The finder's Profile GOALS trim is dropped (see the table).
- **Doctrine:** `GoalProgressLine` is unchanged, and still one component on every surface (SETTLED 2026-08-23). Each row keeps one tap target (§2③); only its destination changes. `view all →` stays beside its trim (§4.2). Home's three stacked lines and the 2026-09-27 hide control are untouched (SETTLED 2026-08-16).

### RCH-11 · Re-tapping the tab you're on does nothing
ux-friction · impact low · effort S · Bottom bar on Home, Cardio, Stats, Coach, Academy

- **Now:** A bar tap calls `onSelect(index)` ([ForgeBottomBar.kt:82-86](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeBottomBar.kt#L82-L86)), which `HubScreen` sends to `goTo` ([HubScreen.kt:129](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/HubScreen.kt#L129)). `goTo` is `animateScrollToPage` ([:85-87](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/HubScreen.kt#L85-L87)), a no-op on the current page. Each page owns its own scroll: Stats ([StatsContent.kt:73](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/StatsContent.kt#L73)), Coach ([CoachScreen.kt:110](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachScreen.kt#L110)), Cardio ([CardioScreen.kt:238](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/cardio/CardioScreen.kt#L238)), Academy ([AcademyScreen.kt:119](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/AcademyScreen.kt#L119), state not hoisted) and Home ([OverviewScreen.kt:442](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L442)). Nothing in the tree or the docs handles a re-tap.
- **Why it matters:** Academy's gallery and Coach's week-by-week account run several viewports, and Stats stacks lenses, heatmap and records. On Android, re-tapping the selected tab to jump to the top is near-universal, and here the tap gives no response at all.
- **Proposal:** In `HubScreen`, when `onSelect` hits `pagerState.currentPage`, emit that tab on a `MutableSharedFlow<BottomTab>(extraBufferCapacity = 1)` provided as `LocalHubReselect`. Each page collects its own tab and scrolls the state it already owns back to the top: `animateScrollToItem(0)` or `animateScrollTo(0)`, or `scrollToItem(0)` under reduce-motion. Academy and Home hoist their state to do this.
- **Doctrine:** Behaviour only: no chrome, badge or element is added (§4.6, SETTLED 2026-08-15 bell and tab-badge rules). §9: reduce-motion is global, so the jump snaps when animations are off.

### RCH-12 · The "hold to go Home" shortcut exists only on Home
discoverability · impact low · effort S · Pushed screens' top-bar back capsule

- **Now:** `goHome` pops to the hub and selects Home ([ForgeNavHost.kt:169-174](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L169-L174)). It is provided as `LocalGoHome` ([:206](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L206)) and read only by the bell's long-press ([NotificationBell.kt:60](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/NotificationBell.kt#L60), [:78-81](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/NotificationBell.kt#L78-L81)). The bell has one call site, Home's top bar ([OverviewScreen.kt:454](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L454)), where going Home does nothing. DESIGN §4.6 still lists "hold→Home" on the bell ([DESIGN.md:100](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L100)), and DECISIONS 2026-07-27 justified removing the wordmark because "the wordmark's go-Home shortcut survives as the bell's long-press, so nothing was actually lost" ([DECISIONS.md:281](../../../.claude/design/DECISIONS.md#L281)). The bell went Home-only the same day, and that made the claim false. The top bar's back capsule is a plain `clickableLabeled` ([ForgeGroups.kt:685-701](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/ForgeGroups.kt#L685-L701), [:767](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/ForgeGroups.kt#L767)).
- **Why it matters:** Stacks like Profile → Gallery → Camera, or Home → History → a session, take one Back per level. The doctrine says a one-gesture way out exists, and there isn't one.
- **Proposal:** Give `ForgeChromeButton` an optional `onLongClick`/`onLongClickLabel` (via `combinedClickable`, so other chrome buttons keep their current press). `ForgeTopBar` passes `LocalGoHome.current` with the label "Go to Home" for its back capsule only. The live session doesn't use `ForgeTopBar` and Settings uses its own `SettingsTopBar`, so neither changes. Move "hold→Home" in DESIGN §4.6 from the bell to the `←`, and drop the bell's dead long-press. If the owner would rather have no hidden gesture, the honest alternative is to delete `goHome` and correct DESIGN §4.6 and DECISIONS 2026-07-27. The About-page half of the original finding is SEP-16 (see the table).
- **Doctrine:** One back affordance per page (§4.6). This adds a hold to the same `←`, not a second control. TalkBack hears it through the long-click label (§14). It restores what DECISIONS 2026-07-27 recorded as kept.

### RCH-10 · The daily reminder and come-back nudge repeat fixed poster lines, with em dashes and an emoji
copy-voice · impact low · effort S · Daily training reminder, come-back nudge

- **Now:** The reminder's bodies are fixed lines with no numbers in them except the streak and the day name ([TrainingReminder.kt:41-67](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/notify/TrainingReminder.kt#L41-L67)):
  - "No workout scheduled today — recovery is where the gains land. Sleep, eat well, come back fresh."
  - "🔥 $streakDays-day streak — keep the chain alive."
  - "$dayName today — ready when you are."

  The come-back nudge opens "No pressure — your plan's right where you left it" ([WeeklyRecapWorker.kt:118-122](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L118-L122)). The em dashes and emoji are already tracked: the 2026-09-26 audit's "copy vs DESIGN §11" note ([11-domain-services-core.md](../2026-09-26/11-domain-services-core.md)) and two allowlist entries ([design-allowlist.txt:111](../../../forge-android/app/src/test/resources/design-allowlist.txt#L111), [:115](../../../forge-android/app/src/test/resources/design-allowlist.txt#L115)). Two things are not tracked. The lines never vary with the user's numbers. And the copy gate checks string literals for em dashes ([DesignDoctrine.kt:504](../../../forge-android/app/src/test/java/com/forge/app/ui/DesignDoctrine.kt#L504)) and exclamation marks, but not emoji. The finder's claim that these files sit outside the gate was wrong; they are scanned and allowlisted.
- **Why it matters:** For anyone who turned the reminder on, these are the app's most-seen strings, and they read like a different product. PRODUCT's voice section is binding.
- **Proposal:** Build each line from data the worker has or can read cheaply: `dayName`, `streakDays`, the day's exercise count, the next training day via `WeeklySchedule.upcomingKey` on rest days, and the last finished session's date for the come-back nudge. Examples:
  - "Rest day. Pull B is next, Thursday."
  - "12-day streak · Pull B is on today."
  - "Pull B today · 6 exercises."
  - "Last session Sep 21. Pull B is next."

  Cut "No pressure", "keep the chain alive", "recovery is where the gains land" and the emoji. Then delete the two allowlist entries, and add an emoji check (extended-pictographic code points in a string literal) to `DesignDoctrine`. The widget's own strings go with HOM-13's rewrite.
- **Doctrine:** PRODUCT voice (binding) and §11: no em dashes or poster clichés, and generated lines vary with the numbers. §8: no decorative emoji. §14: the gate enforces §11. Behaviour, channels and the quiet-hours rule are unchanged.

### RCH-13 · A successful import's feed receipt leads nowhere
discoverability · impact low · effort S · Notifications feed, import result row

- **Now:** A share-to-Avex import writes its result as a system notice ([MainActivity.kt:186](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L186)). Every system notice becomes a RESULT row with the default `NoticeAction.None` ([NotificationFeed.kt:251-260](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L251-L260)). The feed has no action that opens History ([NotificationFeed.kt:31-43](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L31-L43), [NotificationsScreen.kt:87-103](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/notifications/NotificationsScreen.kt#L87-L103)). So "Imported 312 workouts from Strong · 4108 sets." renders passive, and checking those workouts means Back, then Home's RECENT `view all`. (The Settings import path reports through a snackbar instead; SED-05 covers it.)
- **Why it matters:** Right after a migration from another app, the user's question is whether their history arrived intact. The receipt that names the workouts doesn't open them.
- **Proposal:** Add `NoticeAction.OpenHistory`. Set it only when the result is `ImportResult.Success` with `sessions > 0` or `cardioEntries > 0` ([ImportModels.kt:208](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/importer/ImportModels.kt#L208)). Store it under its own notice id, or with a flag beside the text, so failures stay passive. `NotificationsScreen` maps it to a new `onOpenHistory`, wired in `ForgeNavHost` like the other acting rows: `popBackStack()`, then `navigate(Routes.SESSION_HISTORY) { launchSingleTop = true }`. Acting clears the row through the existing `feed.dismiss(id)` ([NotificationFeed.kt:350](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L350)). Backup-restore rows stay passive.
- **Doctrine:** DECISIONS 2026-07-27: the accent `→` marks a row that goes somewhere, and this row now does. It is a whole-row tap with no per-row `×` (§2③). Feed rank and `NoticeKind` order are unchanged. Clearing the passive rows that remain is HOM-04's swipe.

### RCH-14 · No launcher shortcuts for the quick logging jobs
feature-gap · impact low · effort M · Launcher icon long-press

- **Now:** There are no static or dynamic shortcuts anywhere in the phone or wear module (no `android.app.shortcuts` meta-data, no `ShortcutManager` use). Logging a weigh-in is Home → Profile → scroll to BODY → Weight row → `BodyweightLogSheet` ([ProfileScreen.kt:165](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileScreen.kt#L165), [:427-428](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileScreen.kt#L427-L428)), or the daily check-in row while it is open. FEATURES still advertises "Quick-log your bodyweight from the Stats Body tab" ([FEATURES.md:44](../../../.claude/FEATURES.md#L44)), and that lens no longer exists. Logging cardio is the Cardio tab, then "Log cardio". Feasibility catch: the launcher entry is one of the app-icon activity-aliases (23 live, plus every retired one, which SETTLED keeps in the manifest forever), swapped through `PackageManager` ([AndroidManifest.xml:145](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml#L145), [AppIcon.kt:246](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/appicon/AppIcon.kt#L246)). Static shortcuts would have to be repeated on every alias, and dynamic ones are tied to the alias that was enabled when they were published.
- **Why it matters:** Daily weighers and cardio loggers pay several taps and a scroll for a five-second job, and the long-press menu, a standard Android entry point, is empty.
- **Proposal:** Publish three dynamic shortcuts with `ShortcutManagerCompat.setDynamicShortcuts`, re-published on app start and after every icon swap:
  - "Log weight": Profile with the weigh-in sheet open, through a new route argument.
  - "Log cardio": the Cardio tab with the log sheet open, using CAR-02's one-shot open-log request.
  - "Coach brief" while the Coach tab exists, or "Log workout" (`FREESTYLE_LOG`) in freestyle.

  Each one is an intent to `MainActivity` that carries RCH-02's `EXTRA_OPEN`, so it goes through the same lock-aware router. Leave out a "Start session" shortcut: a fixed label can't follow the coach on rest days, and the widget and Home already cover it. Render icons from the `NavIcons`/`SettingsIcons` families. Correct FEATURES.md:44.
- **Doctrine:** OS launcher metadata only; offline, no account (PRODUCT). Nothing is added to any page, so Home's two-question cut (SETTLED 2026-08-16) holds. The app lock is respected through RCH-02's wait. §8 glyph families, never Material stock in content. The icon-alias rules (SETTLED 2026-09-26) are unchanged.
