# Body tracking and progress photos

Scope: the body-log sheets and screens under `ui/profile/` (`BodyweightLogSheet`, `BodyFatLogSheet`, `BodyMeasurement*`, `LeanMassViewModel`) and the progress-photo stack (`MirrorTest*`, `Gallery*`, `ProgressCamera*`, `ProgressPhotoImage`, `BeforeAfterCardRenderer`), plus the routes and repositories they call. 16 raised, 15 kept (13 revised), 1 dropped. Parts cut from kept findings are listed at the end.

Body logging guards its data carefully, but the data has few places to be read or corrected. Bodyweight has no history screen. Weigh-ins and body-fat readings can't be removed, a measurement's history hides behind a long-press, and a photo's weight never says how old it is. The photo flows each stop one step short: the camera takes one shot per trip, compare has no "since last time" pair, and the viewer can't zoom. The gallery itself has been half stock Material and half kit since the 2026-09-26 and 09-27 rebuilds, with no recorded decision, and MAP still describes the screen it replaced.

## Findings

FAILURES *Figure wall*: a detail page drops the figure row ([FAILURES.md:106](../../../.claude/design/FAILURES.md#L106)). §4.2: the Profile row stays a trim with a real destination, the SIZES precedent ([MAP.md:364](../../../.claude/design/MAP.md#L364)). §12: one reading is a list row ([DESIGN.md:304](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L304)). ### BOD-05 · "What changed since last time?" is the one comparison the gallery never offers
