# A beginner's first week (journey)

Scope: one new user's first week, followed across onboarding, Home, the live session's summary sheet, Coach, Academy and the notifications feed (`ui/launch`, `ui/onboarding`, `ui/overview`, `ui/gym/train`, `ui/coach`, `ui/academy`, `ui/notifications`, plus the domain and repo code they call). 14 raised, 9 kept (7 revised), 5 dropped.

Most of the first-week debt sits where one gate hands over to the next. The coach runs on four thresholds: 3 sessions for readiness, 4 for the baseline, more than 4 weighted sessions per lift before it makes a call, and 8 for recovery load. The UI reads some of those live and some from a weekly pass frozen on Monday. So the Coach page goes stale or silent at the point a beginner checks whether it's working. The first generated lines a user reads (the session opinion and the cold-start reason) are generic. Things that should land with a session, such as lessons or a first rest day you can still train on, arrive a day late or not at all. Most fixes are small and reuse components that already exist.

## Findings

  Also:

  - Once the sessions gate has passed, make the "First suggestion" rung's detail name the lift-history gate.
  - In `pendingBanner`, skip a pass whose `holdReason` is a learning hold (`AutoCoachPlanner.isLearningHold`), instead of re-reading the live count.
  - Keep `accountItemCount` in step.

  Revised: CCH-05 ([06-coach-goals.md](06-coach-goals.md)) already hides the rung while `BaselineEntry` is drawn and collapses the "Forming" rows, so this covers only the weeks after that. - **Doctrine:** PRODUCT principle 2 and §4.9: below a gate, show progress toward it, never "not enough data". §12: m is the real gate, and stacked gates show the unfilled one. §11 gives "Ready to coach" as its example verdict. FAILURES "Verdict without a reading" and "Mark echo" both apply. ### FWK-03 · Each day's first session ends on "Consistency is what moves the needle"

### FWK-05 · Home's first-week strip marks the days before you joined as missed
missing-state · impact medium · effort S · Home THIS WEEK anchor and week strip, the first partial week

- **Now:** `WeekStrip` gives every past untrained day the full "missed" fill (alpha 1 for `i < todayIndex`, [SurfaceKit.kt:403-404](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/experiment/SurfaceKit.kt#L403-L404)); in weekday mode only planned rest days are exempt. The anchor reads "$days / ${weeklyTrainingDays} days" against the whole week ([OverviewScreen.kt:613](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L613)). `memberSinceMs` is stamped when onboarding finishes ([SettingsRepository.kt:1477](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/prefs/SettingsRepository.kt#L1477)), but only Profile reads it ([ProfileRepository.kt:110](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/ProfileRepository.kt#L110)).   - Make the strip's TalkBack reading count the same thing as the anchor, e.g. "0 of 1 planned days, joined Thursday". SETTLED Home (2026-08-16, 2026-08-24): no tally, volume or `WeekReadout` comes back. This corrects an existing mark using the strip's own fills, with no new colour.
