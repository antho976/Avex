# Home, hub navigation, notifications, check-in, recap, trophies, widget

Scope: Home (`ui/overview` incl. `components/`, `state/`), the hub pager and bottom bar (`ui/nav`), the notifications feed, the check-in sheet, Recap, Trophies (frozen behind `Features.SHOW_GAMIFICATION`, no findings) and the Glance widget (`widget/`). 17 raised, 15 kept (13 revised), 2 dropped.

Home's structure still matches the 2026-08-16 and 2026-08-22 cuts. Its debt is that the hero has stopped following SETTLED's rule that the button follows the coach: rest days still get the accent fill, freestyle ignores the directive, and the brief the coach computes on every open never reaches the reason line. Around Home, several things exist but can't be reached or changed: Recap has no way in and can't look back, a saved check-in can't be amended, single feed rows can't be cleared, and the week strip doesn't open its days. The widget still works out its own answer and reads as a text dump.

## Findings

### HOM-02 · Rest days and finished days still get the accent-filled hero button
consistency · impact high · effort S · Home hero CTA

- **Now:** Every `TodayDirective.Kind.REST` labels the CTA "Train anyway" ([OverviewScreen.kt:516](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L516)). SETTLED says the opposite: "On REST the action survives but goes outlined ... a filled ember capsule is the app's one 'do this now' signal and a rest day has none" ([SETTLED.md:274](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L274)).
- **Why it matters:** Straight after a workout, Home reads "Done for today" over the brightest button in the app. That is the self-contradiction SETTLED 2026-08-16 recorded as fixed. A four-day lifter sees it three days a week, and again every evening after training.
- **Proposal:** Branch the CTA on `directive?.kind == REST`; resume and no-program still outrank it. On REST, draw "Train anyway" in the page's secondary treatment, the one `HomePlanAction` wears: `surfaceContainerHigh` fill, `onBackground` label, `CellShape`, 56dp minimum ([OverviewScreen.kt:116](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L116)). Hide the hold caption on REST. Resume, TRAIN, CARDIO and LEARN keep `ForgeHeroAction`. Record the chosen treatment in SETTLED's 2026-08-16 entry. It says "outlined", but Home's secondary capsule moved from an outline to the raised fill on 2026-09-27 ([OverviewScreen.kt:121](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L121)). Revised from the original, which proposed an outline.
- **Doctrine:** This implements what SETTLED 2026-08-16 intends: no accent fill on a rest day. The raised fill follows the 2026-09-27 grouped-surface pass, which already restyled the Plan capsule beside it. It keeps the CTA row in one visual language, and either treatment meets SETTLED. Home keeps `CellShape`, not pills (SETTLED 2026-08-22). Both capsules keep the bounce press, so the half-bounce pair (SETTLED 2026-08-22) does not come back.

### HOM-03 · On a routine day the hero's reason repeats one schedule sentence while the computed brief goes unshown
feature-gap · impact high · effort S · Home hero reason line

- **Now:** On an ordinary TRAIN day the reason is "It's what today's schedule calls for." in weekday mode or "It's the next day in your rotation." in sequence mode, every day ([TodayDirective.kt:209](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/coach/TodayDirective.kt#L209)). The day's own content, "<subtitle> · N exercises", renders only while the directive is still null ([OverviewScreen.kt:507](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L507)), so it disappears once the directive loads. `DirectiveRepository` builds a `PreSessionBrief` on every Home open, with a target weight, rep text and intent for each exercise ([DirectiveRepository.kt:153](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/DirectiveRepository.kt#L153), [PreSessionBrief.kt:38](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/coach/PreSessionBrief.kt#L38)). The VM copies it into `state.brief` ([OverviewViewModel.kt:182](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewViewModel.kt#L182)), and no composable reads it. SETTLED 2026-08-16 says TODAY'S TARGETS was "folded into the hero's whisper line" ([SETTLED.md:261](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L261)), but nothing from the brief reaches that line.
- **Why it matters:** The line under "Pull B" is the most-read sentence in the app, and after the first week it says nothing new. A lifter can't see what today asks of them, or that readiness held the loads down, without starting the session. §11 asks for lines that vary with the numbers.
- **Proposal:** When the directive is TRAIN and its reason is only the routine schedule or rotation sentence, replace that line with one built from the brief. Pick the target with the heaviest `targetWeightLb` among those with `coldStart == false`. Render `<name> <weight> × <repsText> · <N> exercises`, with the weight through `WeightFormatter` in the user's lb/kg/st unit, e.g. "Barbell Row 190 lb × 6–8 · 6 exercises". Append "· held under today" or "· push today" only when the brief's load scale moved. Keep the directive's own reason whenever it carries a layoff, a gap of 3 or more days, soreness or readiness. Add a flag on `TodayDirective.Directive` that marks the routine case, rather than string-matching the sentence in the UI. Put the line builder in `OverviewUiStateMapper` with unit tests. Two limits, both revisions of the original: skip cold-start targets, because their seed is wrong for isolation moves (2026-09-26 audit [10, P3 "Latent (unrendered): PreSessionBrief seeds a first-time isolation from compounds"](../2026-09-26/10-coach-adapt-engine-program.md)); and say nothing about "to open", because the engine can reorder the session (TRF-08 in [01-train-flow.md](01-train-flow.md)). Hide the line when the coach is off, along with the directive, once audit 06's `coachEnabled` P3 is settled ([06-common-nav-theme-overview-goals.md](../2026-09-26/06-common-nav-theme-overview-goals.md)).
- **Doctrine:** This delivers what SETTLED 2026-08-16 says already happened, without bringing back TODAY'S TARGETS as a section or list (§4.10). It replaces a line rather than adding one (§4.3 prose budget; Caption stack), adds nothing between the reason and the CTA (the Academy strip stays gone, SETTLED 2026-08-22), and grounds the line in the user's numbers (§11). The 2026-09-26 audit lists the brief under "computed but never shown" and says to wire it or delete it. This is the wiring.

### HOM-01 · The monthly and yearly Recap has no way in

Nothing else in the phone or wear module navigates there. FEATURES lists "Monthly & yearly recaps" as shipped ([FEATURES.md:88](../../../.claude/FEATURES.md#L88)). The only recap a user ever meets is the Monday "Weekly recap" push. That is a different, weekly summary, and its tap opens plain `MainActivity` ([ForgeNotifications.kt:48](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/ForgeNotifications.kt#L48), [WeeklyRecapWorker.kt:164](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WeeklyRecapWorker.kt#L164)). Neither prior audit lists Recap among the unreachable routes; audit 06 names Nutrition, Coach Lab, Timeline and Academy.
- **Why it matters:** A shipped screen fed by every logged session is invisible. At the end of a month there is nowhere to see that month in numbers.
- **Proposal:** Two ways in.
  1. A feed notice. Add `NoticeKind.RECAP` ("Monthly recaps", explainer "Your month in numbers when it closes") after `MILESTONE`, because declaration order is feed rank. On the first open of a new month, if the month before had at least one tracked session, queue an unread id such as `recap.2026-09`, the way `UNREAD_MILESTONES` does. Title it from the closed month ("September: 14 workouts, 3 PRs"). A new `NoticeAction.OpenRecap(month)` pushes `Routes.RECAP` for that month. Opening it clears the row, and `dismiss` re-queues it for Undo. This needs HOM-10; without it the notice would open on a three-day-old October.
  2. A Profile link. Give the "All time" `SectionAnchor` `action = "recaps"`, opening RECAP ([ProfileScreen.kt:283](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileScreen.kt#L283)). Its figures (sessions, volume, sets, PRs) are the lifetime totals of exactly what Recap breaks down by month and year.

  Dropped from the original: sending the weekly push to RECAP, because that push sums up a week and Recap has no week view. If Antho doesn't want Recap at all, the honest alternative is to delete it and its FEATURES line.
- **Doctrine:** §4.6 and DECISIONS 2026-07-27: a notice belongs in the feed, and this is a row, not a strip. It has the same shape as the Academy's lesson notice, which waits behind the bell and clears when opened. §4.2: the Profile link sits beside a trim of its destination, so it is not a bare link. ON THIS DAY does not return to Home or Profile (SETTLED 2026-08-31); the "weekly recap" named in that entry is the push, not this screen. No arrival banner is added.

### HOM-04 · A passive feed row can only be cleared with Clear all, which also sweeps the coach brief and the invites
ux-friction · impact medium · effort S · Notifications feed

- **Now:** Milestone, import, restore and orphan-session rows have `NoticeAction.None`, so tapping them does nothing ([NotificationsScreen.kt:179](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/notifications/NotificationsScreen.kt#L179)), and they never expire. `NotificationFeed.dismiss(id)` already clears one notice and returns its undo ([NotificationFeed.kt:350](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L350)), but the VM exposes only `clearAll()` ([NotificationsViewModel.kt:63](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/notifications/NotificationsViewModel.kt#L63)). The bell's count is `notices.size` ([ForgeNavHost.kt:208](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L208)), so a single "First full month of training" row keeps the badge lit. Clear all also marks the week's brief as seen and dismisses both setup invites for good; MAP says only a section reset brings those back.
- **Why it matters:** To remove one celebration, the user has to sweep away this week's unread brief as well. Otherwise the bell reads 1 forever and stops meaning anything. FIRST_FULL_MONTH fires for almost every user in their first month.
- **Proposal:** Wrap `NoticeRow` in M3 `SwipeToDismissBox` when `notice.dismissible` is true. The active-session and check-in rows stay fixed. Add `NotificationsViewModel.dismiss(id)`: run `feed.dismiss(id)` under `NonCancellable` as `clearAll` does, then call `snackbar.showUndo("Notice cleared") { undo() }`. Give each swipeable row a "Clear" custom accessibility action, because TalkBack has no spoken equivalent of a swipe. Revised: the original's change to what "N WAITING" counts moves to HOM-08's `waiting` flag.
- **Doctrine:** DECISIONS 2026-07-27 names this shape: "If a per-row clear is ever wanted, it has to be a swipe, not a glyph" ([DECISIONS.md:292](../../../.claude/design/DECISIONS.md#L292)). There is no `×` and no nested tap (§2③). Undo goes through the one `SnackbarController` (§8, §12). Rows stay borderless. The feed is a pushed route, not a hub page, so a sideways swipe doesn't fight the pager. That conflict is why SETTLED 2026-08-23 refused a week swipe on Cardio.

### HOM-05 · Home's week strip can't be tapped, unlike every other consistency grid
discoverability · impact medium · effort S · Home THIS WEEK strip

- **Now:** `WeekStrip` is a single merged semantics node with no click handling ([SurfaceKit.kt:392](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/experiment/SurfaceKit.kt#L392)), and Home is its only caller ([OverviewScreen.kt:621](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L621)). Stats' heatmap and Profile's month grid both answer a day tap with `DayLogSheet`, built by the shared `loadDayLog` ([DayLog.kt:59](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/DayLog.kt#L59), [StatsContent.kt:167](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/StatsContent.kt#L167), [ProfileActivityMonth.kt:159](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L159)). RECENT shows only the last three sessions.
- **Why it matters:** "What did I do on Tuesday?" is the question a ✓ raises. Today the answer means leaving Home for Stats or History. A user who has learned to tap a day on Stats gets nothing here.

Profile's grid follows the same rule ("Only lit days call it"). §8 names `DayLogSheet` for consistency grids. This adds no section, figure or weekly tally to Home (SETTLED 2026-08-16, 2026-08-24).

### HOM-08 · Once saved, today's check-in can't be reopened
ux-friction · impact medium · effort M · Daily check-in, via the feed

- **Now:** The check-in row exists only until today's entry is saved: `if (entry?.skipped == false) null` ([NotificationFeed.kt:182](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L182)). Revised to state that doctrine change.
- **Doctrine:** SETTLED 2026-09-01 bans a launch prompt and a second entry under Coach settings. This adds neither and keeps the single entry in the feed. The row shows the reading, not a status word (§4.9). It doesn't count toward the bell, so the badge still means "waiting on you" (§4.6 as narrowed 2026-08-15). The active-session row already stays after you act on it, so "acting clears a row" (DECISIONS 2026-07-27) has a precedent for exceptions.

While `isLoading` it draws `RecapSkeleton` with `ForgeShimmer` ([RecapScreen.kt:62](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/recap/RecapScreen.kt#L62), [RecapScreen.kt:165](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/recap/RecapScreen.kt#L165)), although the data is a local Room read.

- **Doctrine:** Fixes a named failure (Figure wall in FAILURES). The §3 overview toolkit allows 2 to 4 figures plus at least one mark that works at zero. §12: loading draws nothing, `ForgeShimmer` is only for real latency, and every data section leads with a mark. The bars follow §2② (ranked comparison, value over time). Empty tracks are containers, not fake data (Empty by omission), and dated week labels avoid a Machine leak.

### HOM-13 · The widget reads like a text dump and works out "next up" on its own, so it contradicts Home
ui-polish · impact medium · effort M · Home-screen widget

- **Now:** Every `Text` uses Glance's default size, and bold is the only emphasis ([ForgeWidget.kt:172](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/widget/ForgeWidget.kt#L172)). "AVEX", "WORKOUT IN PROGRESS" and the streak are accent-coloured text ([ForgeWidget.kt:173](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/widget/ForgeWidget.kt#L173), [ForgeWidget.kt:182](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/widget/ForgeWidget.kt#L182), [ForgeWidget.kt:242](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/widget/ForgeWidget.kt#L242)). The week is a string of ●/○ characters ([ForgeWidget.kt:155](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/widget/ForgeWidget.kt#L155)), with no today marker and no planned-rest state, unlike Home's `WeekStrip`. The headline comes from `WeeklySchedule.resolveNextUp` ([ForgeWidget.kt:97](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/widget/ForgeWidget.kt#L97)), which throws away whether that day is today. So on a scheduled rest day, after today's session, or after an unwell check-in, the widget still says "PULL B · 6 exercises" and its tap opens that day, while Home says "Rest today" or "Done for today". It also prints `defaultName`, not the user's own day name ([ForgeWidget.kt:204](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/widget/ForgeWidget.kt#L204)). `DirectiveRepository`'s KDoc says it exists so the home widget doesn't re-derive the answer ([DirectiveRepository.kt:23](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/DirectiveRepository.kt#L23)). COACH_V3_PLAN B2 planned exactly that ([COACH_V3_PLAN.md:573](../../../docs/COACH_V3_PLAN.md#L573)) and is marked built ([COACH_V3_PLAN.md:750](../../../docs/COACH_V3_PLAN.md#L750)), but only the wear tile got the directive.
- **Why it matters:** The surface a lifter glances at most disagrees with the app on the days the coach says rest. On the launcher it looks like debug output, not the app. Accent-coloured text fails AA under three of the five accents (§14).
- **Proposal:**
  - **Content.** Take the headline from `DirectiveRepository.today()`. Use `directive.headline` with the user's own day name resolved through `CustomizationRepository`; the same lookup fixes audit 06's P3 for Home. Add one muted line: the directive's `secondary` when there is one ("Pull B is next, on Thursday"), otherwise the day's exercise count.
  - **Tap.** TRAIN carries `EXTRA_START_DAY_KEY = directive.dayKey`. REST, CARDIO and LEARN open plain `MainActivity`. An active session still resumes.
  - **Cost.** `today()` builds a whole-history snapshot, and `snapshotCached()` doesn't cache anything yet (PERFORMANCE_REVIEW P1). Either land that cache first, or have the app store its last computed answer for the widget to read.
  - **Layout.** Replace "AVEX" with a small muted "TODAY" eyebrow. Set the headline at about 22sp in `FontFamily.Serif`, in onBg. Draw the week as seven Glance `Box` cells that mirror `WeekStrip`: done in onBg, today in accent, planned rest as a muted bar, open as `surfaceContainerHighest`. Set the streak in onBg with an accent dot.

  Already tracked elsewhere, so left out: the provider's preview, resize mode and target cells (2026-09-26 audit 12, item 8) and the widget's em dashes (audit 11). Merged with HOM-09.
- **Doctrine:** This extends SETTLED 2026-08-16 (the hero follows the coach) to the one other phone surface that names today's session. The wordmark belongs only to the launch beat and to exported artifacts (SETTLED 2026-07-27, §11), and a widget is neither. §14: no new accent-coloured text, so accent is spent on one mark, today's cell. The week row mirrors `WeekStrip` instead of inventing a new mark (§4.3).

### HOM-17 · "Hold start to skip warmup" shows forever, even when the warm-up is already off
ux-friction · impact low · effort S · Home hero, under the CTA

- **Now:** The caption renders whenever `startsNewGymSession` is true: every user, every day, and on REST too ([OverviewScreen.kt:584](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L584)).
