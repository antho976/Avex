# Interruption, resume and window changes: rotation, process death, backgrounding, midnight/time-zone, landscape, split-screen, tablet/foldable

Scope: `MainActivity` (edge-to-edge, lifecycle, lock arming), `AppLockManager`, onboarding scaffold, `WorkoutSessionService`, Home's resume hero, `WorkoutRepository` session finish, the freestyle logger and its draft, `ForgeNavHost`/`HubScreen` re-entry, the progress camera, day attribution in Stats/Directive/Profile, and window width across every screen. 10 raised, 9 kept (8 revised), 1 dropped.

The data layer survives interruptions well: sets are in Room, the rest timer is persisted, freestyle and onboarding drafts outlive the process, widget taps survive recreation, and midnight re-buckets the day-anchored flows. The debt is in what the window and the clock do to what is on screen. A dark-only, portrait-phone app still takes the phone's light/dark switch, window width and system bars as given, so it gets dark status icons, rebuilds on a theme flip, stretches on wide windows, and draws onboarding under the bars. Long gaps are treated like five-second ones: an overnight open workout, a morning launch, a freestyle log after a kill and a picker trip under app lock. Most fixes are small and reuse a rule the code already has, such as `closeDanglingSegments`, `goHome` with the M-30 request, `launchedSubActivity` and `FreestyleDraft`'s additive fields.

## Findings

### INT-08 · Onboarding draws under the status bar and the 3-button navigation bar
ui-polish · impact medium · effort S · Onboarding, every step (first run)

- **Now:** The window is edge-to-edge ([MainActivity.kt:354](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L354)). Onboarding is composed straight into MainActivity's root `Box` ([MainActivity.kt:601-603](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L601-L603)). `OnboardingScaffold` pads a fixed 24dp at the sides and 28dp top and bottom, with no `WindowInsets` ([OnboardingScaffold.kt:48-53](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/onboarding/OnboardingScaffold.kt#L48-L53)). Nothing under `ui/onboarding` reads an inset. The startup and preferences-failed messages next to it both use `safeDrawingPadding()` ([MainActivity.kt:707](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L707), [:727](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L727)), and every hub page gets its insets from its Scaffold. Two collisions follow:
  - A status bar taller than 28dp, usual on phones with a hole-punch camera, overlaps the back arrow, the step rail and Skip.
  - A 48dp 3-button bar covers about the bottom 20dp of the full-width CTA.

  On the day-count page, the 148dp `PlanLedger` sits outside the scrolling slider ([OnboardingScreen.kt:331-347](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/onboarding/OnboardingScreen.kt#L331-L347)). In a landscape window about 400dp tall, the question and its stepper are left with almost no height. Not checked on a device.
- **Why it matters:** This is the first screen every new user sees. On 3-button phones, a setting common among people with motor impairments, the main button sits partly under system Back/Home. Rotating during setup squeezes the "how many days" stepper out of view.
- **Proposal:** Add `.windowInsetsPadding(WindowInsets.safeDrawing)` to `OnboardingScaffold`'s root `Box`, ahead of its 24/28dp padding, so the padding becomes air inside the safe area. On `PAGE_DAYS`, draw the ledger only when the window is at least about 560dp tall (`LocalWindowInfo.current.containerSize`). Below that, the stepper's own number is the answer. Revised: the finder's `OnboardingScreenshotTest` doesn't exist, and the Robolectric goldens draw no system bars, so check this on a device with 3-button navigation and a hole-punch camera instead.
- **Doctrine:** DESIGN §3 Onboarding says the plan drawn under the question is "real at every stage or absent" ([DESIGN.md:87](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L87)), so hiding it on a short window is allowed. SETTLED 2026-09-25 already limited the ledger to this one page ([SETTLED.md:201](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L201)), and that stays. No chrome is added: no wordmark and no chapter eyebrow. This only gives onboarding the inset handling every other surface already has.

### INT-01 · The dark-only app still follows the phone's light/dark switch: dark status icons in light mode, and a theme flip rebuilds the screen
ui-polish · impact medium · effort S · Status and navigation bars on every screen; open sheets, the photo viewer and typed input when the system theme changes

- **Now:** `enableEdgeToEdge()` is called with its defaults ([MainActivity.kt:354](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L354)). androidx's default for both bars is `SystemBarStyle.auto`, which picks the icon colour from the system's night mode. Nothing else in the app sets bar appearance: there is no `WindowInsetsControllerCompat` and no `isAppearanceLightStatusBars`. The theme's `windowLightStatusBar=false` ([themes.xml:12](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/res/values/themes.xml#L12)) is overwritten by that call. The page ground is near-black (`PearlGradTop` #17120E, [Color.kt:18](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/theme/Color.kt#L18)). So on a phone in light mode, the clock and status icons are drawn dark on dark, and 3-button navigation gets a light contrast scrim.

  Separately, the manifest declares no `configChanges` ([AndroidManifest.xml:105-110](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml#L105-L110)). Nothing reads the system theme (no `isSystemInDarkTheme`, no `values-night`), yet a uiMode change still destroys and recreates the activity. Battery Saver turning dark theme on, Bedtime mode and a sunset schedule all cause one. Each recreation closes the plain-`remember` sheets and drops what was typed, as CardioLogSheet's M-12 note describes for rotation ([CardioLogSheet.kt:86-92](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/cardio/components/CardioLogSheet.kt#L86-L92)). Not checked on a device, and the Roborazzi goldens draw no system bars.
- **Why it matters:** Anyone whose phone is in light mode sees illegible status icons on every screen, every session. Battery Saver kicking in at 15% mid-workout closes the open weigh-in or measurement sheet with no rotation involved.
- **Proposal:**
  1. Call `enableEdgeToEdge(statusBarStyle = SystemBarStyle.dark(Color.TRANSPARENT), navigationBarStyle = SystemBarStyle.dark(Color.TRANSPARENT))` so the bar icons are light in every system mode. Afterwards, look at the hub's bottom bar and onboarding (INT-08) with 3-button navigation.
  2. Add `android:configChanges="uiMode"` to MainActivity. Since nothing reads night mode, handling it in-app changes nothing on screen and stops theme flips from recreating the activity.

  Revised: the status-bar half was found during verification. The finder's step 2 is left to the 2026-09-26 audit, where it is tracked and still open: `rememberSaveable` for the body-log sheets, the gallery, the holiday dialog, Add exercise and the goal editor's search ([03-profile.md:47](../2026-09-26/03-profile.md), [:54](../2026-09-26/03-profile.md), [04 "Rotation drops open Settings dialogs"](../2026-09-26/04-settings-security-notifications.md), [06 goals nit](../2026-09-26/06-common-nav-theme-overview-goals.md), [README theme 9](../2026-09-26/README.md)). This change only removes one of its triggers.
- **Doctrine:** SETTLED Facts: "dark-only (… never build light variants)" ([SETTLED.md:136](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L136)). That is exactly why pinning the dark bar style and handling uiMode in-app is safe. No light variant is built, and nothing inside the app changes.

### INT-05 · After Android reclaims a freestyle log, coming back asks "Resume log or Start fresh" and loses the rest readout
ux-friction · impact medium · effort S · Freestyle logger after process death (switching to music, the camera or a call; freestyle runs no foreground service)

- **Now:** The live log is `FsLogState` on the ViewModel ([FreestyleLogViewModel.kt:203-214](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/freestyle/FreestyleLogViewModel.kt#L203-L214)), so it survives rotation, and the autosaved draft survives process death. After a kill, the nav back stack restores `FREESTYLE_LOG` with a fresh ViewModel that has no `SavedStateHandle` ([FreestyleLogViewModel.kt:62](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/freestyle/FreestyleLogViewModel.kt#L62)). `draftChecked` is false, so the screen loads the draft into `pendingDraft` ([FreestyleLogScreen.kt:131-135](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/freestyle/FreestyleLogScreen.kt#L131-L135)) and renders the `UNSAVED` / "Unfinished workout" prompt. That is the same prompt as for a log the user deliberately walked away from. `activeId` and `lastLoggedAtMs` aren't in the draft ([FreestyleDraft.kt:46](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/freestyle/FreestyleDraft.kt#L46)). After Resume, the header's ` · REST m:ss` ([FreestyleLogParts.kt:104](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/freestyle/FreestyleLogParts.kt#L104)) is gone, and the last exercise opens rather than the one in use ([FreestyleLogScreen.kt:391](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/freestyle/FreestyleLogScreen.kt#L391)).
- **Why it matters:** A freestyle lifter who switches to Spotify between sets can come back to a decision instead of their workout. After resuming, the rest reading has vanished and a different exercise is open. FRE-04 notes freestyle has no foreground service, so this kill is more likely here than in the live session.
- **Proposal:** Inject `SavedStateHandle` into `FreestyleLogViewModel` and write the active `draftId` to it whenever a log starts or a draft is adopted. In the draft-check effect, if the loaded draft's `draftId` matches the saved one, adopt it silently: set items, `draftId` and `openedAtMs`, and leave `pendingDraft` empty. A genuine re-entry has no saved id, so it keeps the prompt. Add optional `"a"` (activeId) and `"l"` (lastLoggedAtMs) keys to `FreestyleDraft.toJson`, additive with no schema bump, the way `"u"` was added ([FreestyleDraft.kt:67](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/freestyle/FreestyleDraft.kt#L67)), and restore them. Revised: the finder's restore of a running hold stopwatch is dropped. Restoring a running hold after a gap of unknown length is the bug the live session already fixed ("stopwatch unattended-run ceiling after process death", [2026-09-26 01-gym-train.md](../2026-09-26/01-gym-train.md)).
- **Doctrine:** No new UI. The resume prompt stays for its real case, so FRE-13's age line on it ([03-freestyle.md](03-freestyle.md)) is unaffected. FRE-04's service and bubble would make this kill rarer but don't remove it. DESIGN §3 Live/flow is function-first, and an interruption shouldn't add a decision. Draft compatibility follows `FreestyleDraft`'s own additive-key precedent.

### INT-06 · Opening the app the next morning lands on whatever deep screen was left last night
ux-friction · impact medium · effort S · Launcher re-entry after hours away (warm process or restored task)

- **Now:** MainActivity is `singleTask` ([AndroidManifest.xml:109](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml#L109)). A launcher tap brings the existing task forward, and `onStart` only re-evaluates the lock ([MainActivity.kt:221-226](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L221-L226)). `rememberNavController` and the hub's `rememberPagerState` are both saveable ([ForgeNavHost.kt:77](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L77), [HubScreen.kt:75](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/HubScreen.kt#L75)), so even a task restored after process death reopens on the last route and tab. Nothing measures how long the app was away. `goHome`, which pops to the hub and selects Home ([ForgeNavHost.kt:171-174](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L171-L174)), is wired only to the bell's long-press ([NotificationBell.kt:60](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/NotificationBell.kt#L60)). RCH-02 fixes where notification and widget taps land ([14-reachability.md](14-reachability.md)). The plain launcher tap has no equivalent.
- **Why it matters:** The next morning, last night's Settings › Backup, a session detail or a lesson greets the user instead of Home's "what do I do now". Getting to today's answer takes several Back presses. On Monday this hides the bell that holds the brief.
- **Proposal:** In `onStop`, skipping config changes and `launchedSubActivity` stops, stamp `leftAtWallMs` and keep it in `onSaveInstanceState`. In `onStart`, raise a sequenced return-home request when the app was away more than 4 hours or a local midnight has passed, the same way `WidgetOpenRequest` is raised ([MainActivity.kt:121](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L121)). `ForgeNavHost` consumes it through `goHome` once `LocalAppLockActive` is false. A pending widget or notification request wins. Fold it into RCH-02's `ExternalOpenRequest` as target `"home"`. Revised: besides `GYM_DAY` and `FREESTYLE_LOG`, never navigate away from a screen that can hold unsaved work: `PROGRAM_BUILDER`, `GOAL_EDITOR` and `PROGRESS_CAMERA` ([Routes.kt:48-53](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/Routes.kt#L48-L53)).
- **Doctrine:** Nothing is drawn: no banner and no dialog, so §4.6 holds and SETTLED's three removed launch dialogs stay removed ([SETTLED.md:89-92](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L89-L92)). It follows §4.1 overview-first and PRODUCT's Monday ritual ([PRODUCT.md:17](../../../PRODUCT.md#L17)). It reuses `goHome` and the M-30 request shape instead of adding a second routing path.

### INT-02 · A workout left open overnight comes back under today's name and finishes as a 12-hour session
missing-state · impact medium · effort M · Home hero in its resume state, the ongoing-workout notification, the feed's IN PROGRESS row

- **Now:** While the day screen stays open (the phone pocketed on the live session), its sitting segment stays open and the foreground service keeps the process alive. The notification reads "<day> · N min", rebuilt every 60 s with no ceiling ([WorkoutSessionService.kt:140](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionService.kt#L140), [:154-158](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WorkoutSessionService.kt#L154-L158)), so by morning it says "612 min". On Home, an open session only swaps the button to "Resume session". The eyebrow still says `TODAY`, and the headline and reason still come from today's directive ([OverviewScreen.kt:495-511](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L495-L511), [:534](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/overview/OverviewScreen.kt#L534)). Monday can therefore say "Push B" over a button that resumes Sunday's Pull A. The feed row says "IN PROGRESS · Pull A · Picks up where you stopped." with no age ([NotificationFeed.kt:216-218](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/NotificationFeed.kt#L216-L218)). Finish stamps `finished_at = now` and closes the open segment at now ([WorkoutRepository.kt:310](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/WorkoutRepository.kt#L310), [:322](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/WorkoutRepository.kt#L322)), so the whole night becomes active time on the next day. The repository already has the right rule: `closeDanglingSegments` and `resolveOrphanSession` end an abandoned sitting at its last logged set ([WorkoutRepository.kt:507](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/WorkoutRepository.kt#L507), [:623-626](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/WorkoutRepository.kt#L623-L626)). They run only after process death, or for a day no longer in the program.
- **Why it matters:** Walking out without tapping Finish is common. The notification nags all night with a climbing count. The next morning Home offers yesterday's workout under today's name, and finishing it records a 12-hour session that skews duration, the week and the streak (which reads finish time, see INT-09).
- **Proposal:**
  1. **Home.** Whenever `activeSessionDayKey` is set, the headline names the open session's day, not the directive's, and the reason line reads "14 sets · last at 10:52 PM". When the session started before today's local midnight, the eyebrow reads `OPEN SINCE SUN` instead of `TODAY`. The filled capsule stays "Resume session" and Plan stays beside it.
  2. **Finish rule, data only.** When the open segment has gone 3 hours or more without a set, `finishInTransaction` takes an end instant. It closes the segment at the last logged set and stamps `finished_at` there, the rule `closeDanglingSegments` already uses.
  3. **Notification and feed.** After 3 hours with no set, the notification stops counting and reads "Open since 9:40 PM · last set 10:52 PM". The feed row's detail uses the same line.

  Revised: the finder's filled "Finish at 10:52 PM" on Home is dropped. It would close the session without the finish summary (rating, journal) and take the Plan slot.
- **Doctrine:** SETTLED's Home rules let resume outrank the coach because it is a fact, and they forbid the page contradicting itself in its two largest elements ([SETTLED.md:270-273](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L270-L273)). Today the headline and the button name different days. The change also follows §11 "a status never stands alone" ([DESIGN.md:283](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L283)) and §12's stale state, the last-known reading with its age ([DESIGN.md:309](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L309)). These are the hero's own states, not a strip, so the 2026-07-27 banner rule holds ([DECISIONS.md:266-275](../../../.claude/design/DECISIONS.md#L266-L275)). The live-session screen (SETTLED Untouchable) is not edited, because the finish rule is data-only. GYM-01's richer notification ([16-gym-floor.md](16-gym-floor.md)) is compatible.

### INT-04 · With App lock on, a file picker, Health Connect grant or notification-settings trip ends on the lock screen
ux-friction · impact low · effort S · App lock gate on return from activities Avex launched itself

- **Now:** `onStop` arms the lock on every stop except a config change ([MainActivity.kt:269-280](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L269-L280)), including a stop caused by an activity Avex launched itself. MainActivity already tracks that case in `launchedSubActivity` ([:152](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L152), [:236-247](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L236-L247)). "Lock again" defaults to Immediately ([SettingsRepository.kt:274](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/prefs/SettingsRepository.kt#L274), [SettingsSecurityPage.kt:12-16](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/settings/SettingsSecurityPage.kt#L12-L16)), so `onForeground` invalidates the session after any trip ([AppLockManager.kt:94-106](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/security/AppLockManager.kt#L94-L106)). The comment at MainActivity.kt:278 accepts this on purpose. The backup save-as callback even asks for the gallery credential after the picker, "because the picker round-trip itself re-locks" ([SettingsScreen.kt:210-212](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/settings/SettingsScreen.kt#L210-L212)). With both locks on, one backup therefore asks twice within seconds. MAP still documents the opposite, that "a picker/camera/share return never re-locks" ([MAP.md:560](../../../.claude/design/MAP.md#L560)), and the Settings row says "After you leave the app" ([SettingsSecurityPage.kt:59](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/settings/SettingsSecurityPage.kt#L59)). Affected trips are save/open and folder pickers, Health Connect consent, the OS notification settings and share targets. Revised: on Android 13+ the system photo picker is a translucent sheet and probably doesn't stop the activity, so progress-photo import is likely unaffected unless it falls back to the full-screen document picker. Not checked on a device.
- **Why it matters:** The privacy-minded people who turned the lock on authenticate again for a trip that doesn't feel like leaving the app. Backups, Health Connect grants and the feed's "turn on notifications" all end on the gate.
- **Proposal:** Call `appLock.onGenuineBackground(selfLaunched = launchedSubActivity)`. When the stop was self-launched, the grace becomes `max(timeoutMs, 60_000)`. Revised: if the screen goes off during the trip, the grace is cancelled (`AppLockManager` registers `ACTION_SCREEN_OFF` while a self-launched trip is pending), so the KDoc's guarantee that a pocketed phone always re-arms stays whole. Home, Recents and every other stop still lock under Immediately. Correct MAP.md:556-560 to the 60-second rule, and add an `AppLockManager` test for the self-launched, screen-off and Home paths. This narrows a deliberate security choice, so it is the owner's call.
- **Doctrine:** There is no UI change, so no SETTLED or FAILURES entry applies. It makes the existing "After you leave the app" copy true and brings MAP back in line with the code. The gallery lock shares the session, so it inherits the same rule.

### INT-07 · On a tablet, an unfolded foldable or a phone in landscape, every screen stretches edge to edge
ui-polish · impact low · effort S · Every screen at widths of 600dp and up

- **Now:** SETTLED records "portrait phone only (no adaptive/tablet/landscape)" ([SETTLED.md:137](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L137)), but the manifest locks nothing ([AndroidManifest.xml:105-110](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml#L105-L110)). With `targetSdk = 36` ([build.gradle.kts:47](../../../forge-android/app/build.gradle.kts#L47)), Android 16 ignores orientation and resizability requests on displays 600dp wide or more anyway, so the app is drawn at those sizes. No content width is capped. Only the arrival banner and the advanced-tracking prompt use `widthIn(max = 420.dp)` ([ArrivalBannerHost.kt:91](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/ArrivalBannerHost.kt#L91), [CoachAdvancedPrompt.kt:92](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachAdvancedPrompt.kt#L92)). Width-driven marks grow with the window:
  - Month and heatmap cells are square off the full width ([ProfileActivityMonth.kt:283](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProfileActivityMonth.kt#L283), [CalendarHeatmap.kt:153](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/components/CalendarHeatmap.kt#L153)), so at 840dp the month grid runs about 650dp tall.
  - Academy sketches use a fixed aspect ratio ([AcademySketches.kt:306](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/AcademySketches.kt#L306)), so one sketch is taller than a landscape phone.
  - The live set slab is full-width ([SetInputRow.kt:355-360](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L355-L360)).

  Every golden is a portrait Pixel 7 ([RecipeScreenshotTest.kt:45](../../../forge-android/app/src/test/java/com/forge/app/ui/RecipeScreenshotTest.kt#L45)).
- **Why it matters:** Fold and tablet owners get a phone design pulled sideways. Filled capsules span the window, the steppers are a long reach apart, and one calendar or lesson sketch fills more than a screen. Few users are affected, but each of them sees it on every screen.
- **Proposal:** Draw one phone-shaped column instead of adaptive layouts. In MainActivity's content `Box` ([MainActivity.kt:601](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/MainActivity.kt#L601)), wrap the onboarding, `ForgeNavHost` and `AppLockScreen` branches in `Box(Modifier.fillMaxSize(), contentAlignment = TopCenter) { Box(Modifier.fillMaxHeight().widthIn(max = 600.dp)) { … } }`. The Pearl gradient that `ForgeTheme` paints behind everything fills the sides. Sheets already cap at M3's 640dp. Don't lock phone orientation: WCAG 1.3.4 counts a forced single orientation as a failure unless it is essential. Add `RecipeScreenshotTest` variants at `w840dp-h900dp` and `w800dp-h360dp`. Revised: impact lowered to low and effort to S, because the cap is a few lines and the goldens reuse the existing harness. Ask the owner first, since it reframes every screen.
- **Doctrine:** This keeps SETTLED's fact: one fixed column, no new layouts and no navigation rail, so nothing adaptive. It follows the 420dp caps the kit already uses. The live-session screen (Untouchable) is wrapped, not edited. No boxes or hairlines are added (§1), because the sides are the existing page ground.

### INT-09 · A workout that crosses midnight lights one day in Profile's activity grid and counts on the next in the streak printed under it
consistency · impact low · effort S · Profile ACTIVITY (grid and streak), Home week strip, History, today's directive and next-up

- **Now:** Day attribution uses two clocks.
  - **Start time:** Profile's ACTIVITY grid ([ProfileRepository.kt:350](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/ProfileRepository.kt#L350)), the week strip ([StatsRepository.kt:173-182](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsRepository.kt#L173-L182)), History's day groups ([HistoryFiltering.kt:24](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/history/HistoryFiltering.kt#L24)) and `TodayDirective.trainedToday` ([TodayDirective.kt:255-258](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/coach/TodayDirective.kt#L255-L258)).
  - **Finish time:** the streak shown under that same grid ([StatsRepository.kt:249-255](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsRepository.kt#L249-L255), [:287-288](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsRepository.kt#L287-L288), [SessionDao.kt:106-118](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/db/dao/SessionDao.kt#L106-L118)), plus both `trainedTodayKeys` filters ([StatsRepository.kt:189-191](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsRepository.kt#L189-L191), [DirectiveRepository.kt:92-96](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/DirectiveRepository.kt#L92-L96)). DirectiveRepository's comment claims finish-anchoring is "like Stats", which is only half true.

  Take a Saturday session and then one from Sunday 23:20 to 00:35. The grid lights Saturday and Sunday, while the streak under it sees Saturday and Monday with a gap. On Monday, next-up treats Sunday's day as trained today, while `trainedToday` says it wasn't.
- **Why it matters:** Late-night and shift-work lifters see one section contradict itself. This is rare, but it reads as wrong without looking broken.
- **Proposal:** Use one rule: a session belongs to the local day it started, which is already the rule for four surfaces, including the History list the user reads. Add `SessionDao.observeTrainedStarts()` (`started_at` where finished and tracked), and feed it to `computeStreak` and `currentStreakDays`. Switch both `trainedTodayKeys` filters to `startedAt`. ### INT-10 · Turning the phone in the progress camera resets the pose and lens and cancels the self-timer
ux-friction · impact low · effort S · Progress camera

- **Now:** `pose`, `lensFront`, `showGrid`, `showGhost` and `timerOn` are plain `remember` ([ProgressCameraScreen.kt:90-94](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProgressCameraScreen.kt#L90-L94)). The 3-second countdown runs in `rememberCoroutineScope` ([:82](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProgressCameraScreen.kt#L82), [:150](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProgressCameraScreen.kt#L150)), which is cancelled when the activity recreates, and the route pins no orientation. Propping the phone sideways on a bench, or tilting it while backing away during the timer, recreates the activity. The pose snaps to Front, so the shot is tagged and ghosted as Front. The lens returns to the front camera, the toggles reset and the countdown stops silently. The ghost is crop-filled to the window ([:169](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/profile/ProgressCameraScreen.kt#L169)), so in landscape a portrait reference can't line up with the live frame.
- **Why it matters:** Someone setting up a rear-camera Side or Back shot loses the pose and lens the moment the phone turns, and the timer never fires. A shot taken anyway is filed under the wrong pose, which spoils the same-pose ghost and compare.
- **Proposal:** Pin this route to portrait with a `DisposableEffect` that sets `LocalActivity.current?.requestedOrientation = SCREEN_ORIENTATION_PORTRAIT` and restores the previous value on dispose. Phones honour it. Android 16 ignores it at 600dp and wider, where the window is large enough anyway. Make `pose` (by name), `lensFront`, `showGrid`, `showGhost` and `timerOn` `rememberSaveable`, so a fold, resize or (before INT-01) a theme flip also keeps them. BOD-02 carries the lens and pose across trips ([08-body-photos.md](08-body-photos.md)), and BOD-13 owns the timer length.
- **Doctrine:** SETTLED's portrait-only fact ([SETTLED.md:137](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L137)). Pinning only this route fits WCAG 1.3.4's "essential" exception, because progress shots must match the portrait framing of earlier ones for the ghost and compare to work. INT-07 deliberately leaves the rest of the app unlocked. No chrome changes.
