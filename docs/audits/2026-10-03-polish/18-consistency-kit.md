# Cross-surface consistency and the shared kit

Scope: the shared kit (`ui/common/**`, `ui/theme/**`), every `ui/` package swept for re-implementations of it, and the `ui/experiment` fork that Home, Profile, Goals and Freestyle import. 17 raised, 10 kept (8 revised), 7 dropped. Parts cut from kept findings are listed with the dropped ones.

The 2026-09-27 grouped-surface kit unified forms, lists and top bars, but its own chrome presses with a ripple while the primary capsule beside it bounces. It also stopped short of the pieces every feature needs at its edges: a confirm dialog, a sheet scaffold, a range pager and a quiet link. Each feature re-rolled those, and the copies drifted in button style, fill, contrast and RTL handling. The second pattern is cross-screen rules computed per screen: Week starts, haptic strength, delta colour and "when was this" each hold on some tabs and not others. Many single-screen symptoms are already filed in the area reports. What this area keeps are the kit-level fixes that close several of those at once and stop the drift coming back.

## Findings

### KIT-01 · The kit's chrome and secondary capsules ripple while the primary beside them bounces

### KIT-11 · Five haptics bypass `forgeHaptic`, so Haptic feedback "Off" still buzzes mid-session
consistency · impact medium · effort S · Rest-timer bubble tap and hold, timed-hold stopwatch, hold to repeat the last set, session summary PR buzz

- **Now:** `HapticExt` says "every event haptic in the app goes through here, never straight to the platform", and plays nothing for "off" ([HapticExt.kt:13-20](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/HapticExt.kt#L13-L20)). The rest bubble buzzes on tap (which only opens its controls) and on hold, straight through `LocalHapticFeedback` ([RestTimerBubble.kt:215-222](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/RestTimerBubble.kt#L215-L222)). `SetInputRow` does the same when the hold stopwatch starts or stops and on hold-to-repeat the last set ([SetInputRow.kt:191](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L191), [:579-582](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L579-L582)). None of the four reads `hapticStrength`. The summary sheet checks "off" but always plays LongPress, whatever strength was picked ([SessionSummarySheet.kt:79-85](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SessionSummarySheet.kt#L79-L85)). - **Why it matters:** Someone who turned haptics off, for a quiet gym or for sensory reasons, is still buzzed each time they tap the rest timer or repeat a set. Someone on Light gets the strongest pulse at the end of a PR session.
- **Proposal:** Replace the raw calls with `view.forgeHaptic(type, LocalForgeSettings.current.hapticStrength)`: COUNTDOWN_TICK for the stopwatch start and stop, SET_LOGGED for repeat-last-set, PR_OR_FINISH for the summary. - **Doctrine:** §9 "Haptics rare (`forgeHaptic`): set logged, PR/finish, timer ticks" and nothing else ([DESIGN.md:252](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L252)), and §15's Live checklist. The live-session screen is untouchable for layout ([SETTLED.md:138](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L138)); this changes no layout. §16: a mechanical rule becomes a test. Audit 01's P4 list names the summary's raw call ([01-gym-train.md:193](../2026-09-26/01-gym-train.md)); this covers the other four and adds the gate.
