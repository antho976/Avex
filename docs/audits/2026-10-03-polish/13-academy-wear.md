# Academy and the Wear OS app

Scope: the Academy tab and lesson reader (`ui/academy/`), the watch app (`:wear` `ui/`, `data/`, `glance/`, `MainActivity`) and the phone-side bridge (`service/wear/`). 17 raised, 16 kept (10 revised), 1 dropped.

Most of the debt is on the watch. The wrist was built to mirror the phone, but it computes its own set target, and it has no drawn state for the moments around a set: the end of the day, a timed hold, a paused rest, a lost phone and a lowered wrist. Tiles and complications don't open anything, and the idle screen answers "today" differently from the Today tile. Every control except Log set and ± is small bare text. The Academy rebuild of 2026-09-26 is clean against the doctrine. What it lacks is how readers get in and out (Home's LEARN button, the reader's Next chain), and calculators that start from the reader's own numbers.

## Findings

### ACW-01 · The watch offers a different set target than the phone, and never the coach's step
consistency · impact high · effort M · Watch set view (weight figure and reps line)

- **Now:** The phone fills set N with your weight and reps from set N last time ([SetInputRow.kt:132](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L132), [ExerciseCard.kt:363](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/ExerciseCard.kt#L363)). With no earlier set, reps start at the top of the range: "8-12" gives 12 ([ExerciseCard.kt:43](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/ExerciseCard.kt#L43)). It also shows the coach's `ProgressionAdvisor` step as a chip ([DayViewModelBuilders.kt:122](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/DayViewModelBuilders.kt#L122), [DayViewModelBuilders.kt:234](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/DayViewModelBuilders.kt#L234)). The mirror's weight is this session's latest set, or else the last set of last time, picked by `maxByOrNull { setIndex }`, which is usually a back-off set ([WatchSessionMirror.kt:127](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WatchSessionMirror.kt#L127), [WatchSessionMirror.kt:193](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WatchSessionMirror.kt#L193)). Its reps are the plan's range text ([WatchSessionMirror.kt:166](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WatchSessionMirror.kt#L166)), which the watch cuts to the bottom of the range with `takeWhile { isDigit }`, so "8-12" gives 8 ([SessionScreen.kt:63](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/SessionScreen.kt#L63)). The mirror's comment says the coach's chip "stays a phone surface". The plan wanted the target to come "from the same suggestion the phone chips show" ([WEAR_OS_PLAN.md:141](../../WEAR_OS_PLAN.md#L141)).
- **Why it matters:** This hits every set for every watch user. Someone who did 11 reps last week is offered 8, and one tap of Log set records 8. The progression the Academy teaches (reps first, then weight) stalls, and someone who logs from the watch never sees the coach's step.
- **Proposal:**
  1. Match the phone (S, no protocol change). In `WatchSessionMirror`, take the earlier set at `lastPerformanceSets(effectiveId)[setIndex - 1]` and publish its weight and its reps as `targetWeightText` and `targetRepsText`. With no such set, keep today's weight fallback and send the top of the rep range, as `targetRepsOf` does.
  2. Add the coach's step (M). Move the `suggestNextLoad` / `suggestNextReps` input assembly out of `DayViewModelBuilders` into a repository function. `SetLogUseCase` already resolves the slot without a ViewModel. Publish the result as additive `SessionLiveDto` fields with defaults: `suggestedWeightText`, `suggestedReps` and `fromWeightText`. When there is a step, the figure starts on it, and the SET line shows where it came from ("SET 2 OF 4 · +5 FROM 185"). The bezel and ± still override it. This adds no new control to the round screen. It makes the coach's step the one-tap default on the watch, while the phone keeps it behind a chip, so Antho should approve step 2 before it is built.
  Revised: the original drew the step as a second tappable line under the figure. That screen already has no spare height.
- **Doctrine:** WEAR.md: the serif figure is the adjust target, one decision per screen ([WEAR.md:26](../../../.claude/design/WEAR.md#L26)). PRODUCT principle 2, show the reading and not only the verdict: "+5 FROM 185" carries the number the step came from ([PRODUCT.md:123](../../../PRODUCT.md#L123)). Principle 1 holds: a single set's load is not a program change, and the bezel overrides it. The new protocol fields have defaults, as `publishedAtMs` and `CmdAckDto.kind` did. Nothing in SETTLED removed a wrist suggestion.

### ACW-02 · Log set stays live after the day is done and on timed holds, so a tap adds a stray set or is refused
missing-state · impact high · effort M · Watch set view at the end of a session, and on plank or hold slots

- **Now:** Once every planned slot is logged, the resolver stays on the last logged slot ([CurrentSlotResolver.kt:41](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/CurrentSlotResolver.kt#L41)). The mirror clamps `setIndex` to the plan ([WatchSessionMirror.kt:163](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WatchSessionMirror.kt#L163)), so the watch shows "SET 3 OF 3" with every tick filled above a white "Log set" ([SessionScreen.kt:222](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/SessionScreen.kt#L222), [SessionScreen.kt:244](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/SessionScreen.kt#L244)). `SetLogUseCase` has no planned-count guard, so a tap writes a fourth, unplanned set and starts a rest ([SetLogUseCase.kt:109](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/session/SetLogUseCase.kt#L109)). On a timed hold the DTO has no timed flag ([WearDtos.kt:16](../../../forge-android/shared/src/main/kotlin/com/forge/shared/protocol/WearDtos.kt#L16)), so the same filled button shows, and every tap is refused with "log timed holds on the phone" in an 8sp line ([SetLogUseCase.kt:133](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/session/SetLogUseCase.kt#L133)).
- **Why it matters:** Every session led from the watch ends on a screen that asks for another set. A stray tap while walking to the phone adds a junk set to volume, e1RM and PR reads. In a program with holds, every hold set hits a button that can't work.
- **Proposal:**
  - **End of day.** Add `dayComplete: Boolean = false` to `SessionLiveDto`, set in the mirror when every slot is done, finished early or skipped. In that state, `SetView` keeps its eyebrow, shows a mono "ALL SETS LOGGED" over a serif `figureSmall` "Done", and one muted line, "Finish on your phone". The steppers are hidden, and Log set becomes the unfilled `WristCapsule` "Extra set".
  - **Timed holds.** Let the watch log them, since `SetLogUseCase` says a duration on the wire is what's missing. Add `durationSeconds: Int? = null` to `LogSetCommand` and `isTimed` / `targetHoldSeconds` to `SessionLiveDto`. `SetLogUseCase` writes the set the way the phone's timed path does (reps 0, `durationSeconds`). On a timed slot the figure is the hold ("45s"), ± and the bezel step it by 5 s like the phone's stepper, it starts from last time's hold for this set, and Log set logs it.
  - **Interim, if the protocol change waits.** Draw a timed slot as passive: the plan's hold text as the figure, no steppers, no filled button.
  Revised: the original ended the timed state on "Log holds on your phone". WEAR.md bans pointing the user to the phone.
- **Doctrine:** WEAR.md: "Nothing fake-tappable", status lines sit above the capsule, and guard rails never say "do it on the phone" ([WEAR.md:33](../../../.claude/design/WEAR.md#L33)). FAILURES "Fake tap" ([FAILURES.md:176](../../../.claude/design/FAILURES.md#L176)). The end-of-day pointer matches the Finished beat's existing "on your phone" line ([WearRoot.kt:174](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/WearRoot.kt#L174)), which WEAR.md specifies. No filled button is added. The fields are additive with defaults.

### ACW-03 · "undo" sits next to "rate →", the reps toggle doesn't look tappable, and status text is 8sp
accessibility · impact medium · effort S · Watch set view, rest timer, RPE picker

- **Now:** After a set, "undo" and "rate →" are the same accent 10sp text, 10dp apart ([SessionScreen.kt:267](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/SessionScreen.kt#L267), [TimerView.kt:95](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/TimerView.kt#L95)). Compose widens small tap targets toward 48dp, but these two overlap, so a near miss on "rate" goes to whichever is closer, and that can delete the set. The only way to adjust reps is to tap the line under the figure. It is bare onBg text with 4dp padding and nothing that marks it as a control ([SessionScreen.kt:193](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/SessionScreen.kt#L193)). `WearType.labelSmall` is 8sp ([WearTheme.kt:60](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/WearTheme.kt#L60)), under WEAR.md's 10–13sp band. That style sets the status line ("Not logged · reconnecting", refusal reasons, [SessionScreen.kt:235](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/SessionScreen.kt#L235)), "· retry" ([WearComponents.kt:126](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/WearComponents.kt#L126)) and "close" ([SessionScreen.kt:362](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/SessionScreen.kt#L362)). None of these clickables declares a role or a click label.
- **Why it matters:** These controls are used mid-set with sweaty hands. The one line that says a set did not log is the smallest type on the screen. Most users never find out they can change reps on the watch.
- **Proposal:** Draw "undo" in onBg, not accent, so the destructive one doesn't match the accent nav link "rate →", and put at least 24dp between them. Draw the reps/weight toggle inside a compact unfilled surface capsule (the `WristCapsule` #1A1613 ground, 6dp vertical padding). Raise `labelSmall` to 10sp and `label` to 11sp, and set the status line at `label` in onBg. Give every text control `clickable(onClickLabel = …, role = Role.Button)`. Check the set view still fits the smallest supported round screen, since the column doesn't scroll. Revised: the original's "about 24dp target" overstated it, because Compose's touch-target expansion already applies, so the 48dp boxes it proposed would only take height the round screen doesn't have. Impact lowered from high.
- **Doctrine:** WEAR.md: mono labels 10–13sp ([WEAR.md:22](../../../.claude/design/WEAR.md#L22)), and surfaces only on interactive elements. The toggle is interactive, so its capsule is allowed (§1). DESIGN §2③: accent mono with an arrow means navigation, and undo isn't navigation. No filled button is added.

### ACW-04 · The rest screen doesn't say what's next, and a paused rest looks frozen
feature-gap · impact medium · effort S · Watch rest countdown

- **Now:** `TimerView` collects `repo.session` but uses it only for undo's session id ([TimerView.kt:48](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/TimerView.kt#L48)). The mirror moves to the next set the moment one lands, so during rest the next exercise, weight and reps are already on the watch. The screen draws only REST, the countdown and +30/Skip ([TimerView.kt:83](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/TimerView.kt#L83)). - **Why it matters:** Rest is when you load plates and walk to the next station. The watch has the answer but doesn't show it until the rest ends or you skip it. A paused rest looks broken.
- **Proposal:** Between the figure and the +30/Skip row, add one mono line, two lines at most on a round screen, built from `session`: "NEXT · SET 3 OF 4 · 185 × 8" on the same lift, or "NEXT · BENCH PRESS · 185 × 8" on a new one. When `timer.paused` is true and `pausedRemainingSeconds > 0`, the eyebrow reads "PAUSED" and the ring is drawn in muted onBg. Revised: the original put the line under the capsules, where WEAR.md says text clips on a round screen. Impact lowered from high.
- **Doctrine:** §11: something still to come is worded forward, under NEXT. WEAR.md: status lines sit above the capsule ([WEAR.md:33](../../../.claude/design/WEAR.md#L33)), and the countdown stays the one serif figure. A line of text is not a decision, so one decision per screen still holds.

### ACW-05 · The watch receives "exercise 3 of 6" but never shows it
feature-gap · impact medium · effort S · Watch set view eyebrow

- **Now:** The mirror fills `exerciseIndex` and `exerciseCount` every time ([WatchSessionMirror.kt:161](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WatchSessionMirror.kt#L161)), and nothing in `:wear` reads them. ### ACW-08 · Tapping a tile or complication does nothing
discoverability · impact medium · effort S · Today and Week tiles; load, next-session and rest-timer complications

- **Now:** Neither tile's root `Box` has a `Clickable` ([WearTiles.kt:74](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/glance/WearTiles.kt#L74)), and none of the three complication services calls `setTapAction` ([WearComplications.kt:39](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/glance/WearComplications.kt#L39)). Nothing in `:wear` builds a `LaunchAction` or a tap `PendingIntent`. The plan promised "one-tap open" from the Today tile ([WEAR_OS_PLAN.md:311](../../WEAR_OS_PLAN.md#L311)). During a session, the OngoingActivity chip is the only way back into the app.
- **Why it matters:** Tiles and complications are the parts of the app watch owners see most, and all of them are dead ends. A rest-timer complication that can't open the timer looks broken.
- **Proposal:** Build one `PendingIntent` to `MainActivity`. Attach it to each tile's outer `Box` as `ModifiersBuilders.Clickable` with a `LaunchAction`, and to every complication, including the "—" placeholders, with `setTapAction`. The app already opens on the right screen for the moment: idle glance, set view or timer.
- **Doctrine:** §4.5: aggregate visuals answer a tap ([DESIGN.md:99](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L99)). On a watch face a complication looks tappable by convention, so one that does nothing is FAILURES "Fake tap". No content is added.

### ACW-09 · The watch's idle screen and the Today tile give different answers for today
consistency · impact medium · effort S · Watch idle home vs Today tile

- **Now:** The Today tile leads with the coach's directive headline, then readiness LOAD, then NEXT ([WearTiles.kt:139](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/glance/WearTiles.kt#L139)). The publisher sends the directive so the watch shows "the SAME answer the phone does" ([WearStatePublisher.kt:159](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WearStatePublisher.kt#L159)). `IdleScreen` never reads `directiveHeadline`. It shows LOAD, or else `nextDayTitle ?: "Rest"` ([WearRoot.kt:198](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/WearRoot.kt#L198)). `nextDayTitle` is always null in freestyle and with no plan ([WearStatePublisher.kt:172](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WearStatePublisher.kt#L172)).
- **Why it matters:** Opening the app from the tile changes the verdict, for example from "Rest today" to "Push A". A freestyle lifter below the readiness gates sees "Rest" every day, in the app and on the tile alike.
- **Proposal:** Move the tile's order of precedence (directive, then LOAD, then NEXT) into one function in the glance package, such as `TodayHeadline.from(glance)`, and use it in both `TodayTileService.layout` and `IdleScreen`. On the idle screen, the directive headline is the `figureSmall` with a TODAY caption, and LOAD moves to its secondary line. When `nextDayTitle` and the directive are both null, both surfaces show the week count instead of "Rest".
- **Doctrine:** §4.3: one answer per question. WEAR.md: tiles fall back to the next day plus readiness and are never blank ([WEAR.md:41](../../../.claude/design/WEAR.md#L41)). COACH_V3_PLAN B2: the watch uses the directive word for word. Nothing is added.

### ACW-10 · The watch glances keep yesterday's answer after midnight while the phone app stays alive
missing-state · impact medium · effort S · Today and Week tiles, watch idle home

- **Now:** `/glance/today` is republished when the phone process starts, when a watch pairs and when a session ends ([WearStatePublisher.kt:85](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WearStatePublisher.kt#L85), [WearStatePublisher.kt:145](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/wear/WearStatePublisher.kt#L145), [ForgeApp.kt:95](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ForgeApp.kt#L95)). A phone process killed overnight therefore republishes whenever any worker wakes it. A process that stays alive past midnight does not, and Android often keeps one. `WidgetMidnightWorker` runs at every local midnight for the same kind of staleness on the home-screen widget, but it never touches the watch ([WidgetMidnightWorker.kt:34](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/service/WidgetMidnightWorker.kt#L34)).
- **Why it matters:** Someone who checks the watch before the phone wakes up to "DONE FOR TODAY · 9H AGO". On Monday the Week tile still counts last week. The age stamp is honest, but the headline over it is wrong.
- **Proposal:** In `WidgetMidnightWorker.doWork`, after `refreshForgeWidgets`, get `WearStatePublisher` through a Hilt `EntryPoint` and call `publishGlanceNow()`. It already returns early when no watch is paired. Revised: the original also published after the weekly brief, which isn't needed because the Monday midnight run covers it. It also didn't note that a cold process start already republishes.
- **Doctrine:** WEAR.md: tiles are stamped with the data's age and never claim more than they know ([WEAR.md:41](../../../.claude/design/WEAR.md#L41)). DESIGN §12 stale state. No UI change. It reuses the existing worker and publisher.

### ACW-11 · The back gesture on the RPE picker exits the app instead of going back
ux-friction · impact medium · effort S · Watch RPE picker

- **Now:** The RPE picker is a state flag inside `WearRoot`, not a destination ([WearRoot.kt:54](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/WearRoot.kt#L54), [WearRoot.kt:133](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/WearRoot.kt#L133)). `:wear` has no `BackHandler` or `SwipeToDismissBox` anywhere, and the theme keeps DeviceDefault swipe-to-dismiss. So a swipe right, or the Galaxy back key, closes `MainActivity` and drops the rating instead of returning to the timer. The only way back inside the app is "close", a muted 8sp word ([SessionScreen.kt:362](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/SessionScreen.kt#L362)).
- **Why it matters:** The standard Wear back gesture throws the user out of the workout app mid-rest. To get back they have to find the ongoing chip.
- **Proposal:** Wrap the RPE layer in wear-compose's `SwipeToDismissBox` with the timer or set view as its background, plus `BackHandler(enabled = rpeSetId != null) { rpeSetId = null }` for the hardware key. Then remove "close".
- **Doctrine:** WEAR.md: "swipe-dismiss preserved (no custom back)" ([WEAR.md:26](../../../.claude/design/WEAR.md#L26)). "close" is the custom back control that rule rules out.

### ACW-06 · The ambient mode WEAR.md specifies was never built, so the full UI stays lit for the whole session
feature-gap · impact medium · effort M · Watch app during a session (wrist lowered)

- **Now:** WEAR.md specifies ambient mode: mono only, onBg at 0.6, no accent, no surfaces, safe from burn-in, with the timer keeping its countdown ([WEAR.md:35](../../../.claude/design/WEAR.md#L35)). The W1 plan listed an "ambient timer" ([WEAR_OS_PLAN.md:364](../../WEAR_OS_PLAN.md#L364)). Nothing in `:wear` observes ambient. Instead, `MainActivity` holds `FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON` for the whole session and rest ([MainActivity.kt:59](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/MainActivity.kt#L59)). The accent ring, the white Log set button and the PR wash stay at full brightness for an hour.
- **Why it matters:** Every session costs battery and risks AMOLED burn-in. Lowering the wrist never gives the quiet countdown the design promised.
- **Proposal:** Add `androidx.wear:wear` and register an `AmbientLifecycleObserver` in `MainActivity`. Expose `isAmbient` through a CompositionLocal, and drop `FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON`, since ambient is what keeps the app in front when the wrist is down. In ambient, `TimerView` draws only the countdown in onBg at 0.6, in whole minutes ("2 MIN"), because ambient redraws about once a minute and frozen seconds would be wrong. `SetView` draws only the exercise name and target figure in onBg at 0.6. There are no buttons, accent, ring or filled ticks. The rest-done buzz is unaffected. Revised: the original kept keep-screen-on as an option and a 1dp ring in ambient. WEAR.md says everything but the countdown drops.
- **Doctrine:** This builds WEAR.md's Ambient bullet as written and brings back nothing that was removed. The ambient switch is a state change, not an animation (§9).

### ACW-07 · A lost phone connection shows nothing until a set fails, and +30 and Skip fail silently
missing-state · impact medium · effort M · Watch set view and rest timer

- **Now:** The repository checks for connected nodes only when it sends ([WearDataRepository.kt:554](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/data/WearDataRepository.kt#L554)) and exposes no reachability state. With the phone in a locker, the watch keeps showing the last mirror in full accent with no age. The first sign of trouble is "Not logged · reconnecting", four seconds after a Log set ([SessionScreen.kt:116](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/SessionScreen.kt#L116)). +30 and Skip pass no result callback ([WearDataRepository.kt:369](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/data/WearDataRepository.kt#L369)), so out of range nothing changes and nothing is said.
- **Why it matters:** Bluetooth drops are common in a gym. The lifter trusts a screen that has quietly stopped updating, and taps that look like they work do nothing.
- **Proposal:** The phone declares an `avex_phone_app` capability, mirroring the watch's `avex_wear_app`. The watch exposes `phoneReachable: StateFlow<Boolean>` from a `CapabilityClient` listener with `FILTER_REACHABLE`, and records when reachability was lost. While it is false during a session, the eyebrow reads "RECONNECTING · 2 MIN AGO" and `wearColors` maps the accent to onBg. A Log set, +30 or Skip tapped then shows "Phone out of reach" above the button straight away, instead of after four seconds. Revised: the original's "Sends when your phone is back" was false. A pending Log set is resent only when the user taps again, and nothing resends it automatically.
- **Doctrine:** WEAR.md: a lost connection says "Reconnecting" with last-known data and its age, never an error banner ([WEAR.md:40](../../../.claude/design/WEAR.md#L40)), and a stale state is mono only ([WEAR.md:19](../../../.claude/design/WEAR.md#L19)). DESIGN §12 stale state.

### ACW-13 · The "Your bodyweight" and "Your working weight" calculators start on someone else's numbers
feature-gap · impact low · effort S · Lesson reader: protein and warm-up calculators

- **Now:** For everyone, `WarmupCalculator` starts at 100 kg or 225 lb, and `ProteinCalculator` starts at 80 kg or 180 lb ([AcademyFigures.kt:353](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/AcademyFigures.kt#L353), [AcademyFigures.kt:381](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/AcademyFigures.kt#L381)). Their labels say "Your". `LessonViewModel` loads only readiness ([LessonViewModel.kt:90](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/LessonViewModel.kt#L90)). The app stores the latest bodyweight ([BodyweightDao.kt:20](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/db/dao/BodyweightDao.kt#L20)) and every working set.
- **Why it matters:** The two lessons that promise to be about the reader open on a stranger. Readers have to drag to their own weight before the figure means anything.
- **Proposal:** `LessonViewModel` loads `figureSeeds`: the latest bodyweight in lb, and the heaviest working set of the most recent finished session. Pass them through `ReaderScreen` → `LessonBlockView` → `LessonFigure` ([LessonBlocks.kt:107](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/LessonBlocks.kt#L107)). Set each slider's starting position from its seed, clamped to the range, and keep today's start as the fallback. Revised: the original's optional new Example block for the Volume lesson is dropped, because it adds prose. Impact lowered from medium.
- **Doctrine:** §2①: the reader's own number. The figures' "illustrative shape, never a claim about the reader" rule ([AcademyFigures.kt:73](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/AcademyFigures.kt#L73)) is untouched, since only where the slider starts changes. No ghost data: the seed is real, and the fallback stays as it is today. No prose is added.

### ACW-14 · The lesson's Next ignores what you've read, and the last lesson is a dead end
ux-friction · impact low · effort S · Lesson reader, end of page

- **Now:** `nextAfter` returns the next lesson in the registry whether or not it has been read, and returns null after the last one, Intervals ([LessonViewModel.kt:73](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/LessonViewModel.kt#L73)). `NextBlock` draws it in full onBg, with a Read cue ([Reader.kt:188](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/Reader.kt#L188)). The contents list mutes read titles ([AcademyScreen.kt:328](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/AcademyScreen.kt#L328)), but the reader's Next block does not. A reader who comes in mid-chapter from a "For you" notice is offered what follows, and at the end of the page gets no onward link while earlier lessons are still unread.
- **Why it matters:** Reading forward offers lessons you've already finished, then stops while there is still something unread.
- **Proposal:** Pass `academyRepo.states()` into `nextAfter`. Return the first unopened lesson after this one in page order, wrapping to the start, and skip the current lesson, since its own open is being recorded at the same time. The heading reads "Next in Training", "Next chapter: Cardio" or "Still unread", the last when the search wraps. Return null only when every lesson has been opened.
- **Doctrine:** This keeps page order, which `NextPiece` already uses, and Training is "Best read in order". Nothing is scored. The reading chain still replaces itself on the back stack ([ForgeNavHost.kt:419](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/nav/ForgeNavHost.kt#L419)), so there is still one back.

### ACW-16 · Whether a lesson is read is shown by colour only, so TalkBack can't hear it
accessibility · impact low · effort S · Academy contents rows and the reader's next-lesson row

- **Now:** A read lesson is shown only by a muted title ([AcademyScreen.kt:328](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/AcademyScreen.kt#L328)). `ContentsRow` and the next-lesson row are plain `bounceClick` ([AcademyScreen.kt:318](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/AcademyScreen.kt#L318), [Reader.kt:203](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/Reader.kt#L203)), and `ReadCapsule` clears its own semantics ([Reader.kt:233](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/academy/Reader.kt#L233)). TalkBack hears the title, summary and minutes, with no read/unread state and no action label.
- **Why it matters:** Screen-reader users can't tell which lessons they have read. Sighted users get that from the muted titles.
- **Proposal:** Add `semantics { stateDescription = if (opened) "Read" else "Not read" }` to `ContentsRow` and the next-lesson row. Switch both to `bounceCombinedClick(onClickLabel = "Open lesson")`. Revised: the original also swapped `ReadCapsule` for the outlined `ForgeRowPill`. That is dropped, because the filled secondary button came from the app-wide grouped-surface pass of 2026-09-27, which retired pill rows (commit `46edf5c`). It also appears once per block, not once per row, so it is not a button wall.
- **Doctrine:** PRODUCT accessibility requirements ([PRODUCT.md:135](../../../PRODUCT.md#L135)). §14: semantics on interactive elements that aren't Buttons. A state description isn't a score, so "nothing is scored" still holds.

### ACW-17 · The watch asks for every permission on launch, before anything explains why
ux-friction · impact low · effort S · Watch app first opens

- **Now:** Every `MainActivity.onCreate` asks for heart rate, activity recognition and notifications in one batch whenever any is missing ([MainActivity.kt:43](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/MainActivity.kt#L43)). That happens on the idle glance, before any session exists, with no explanation on the watch. A refusal is asked again on the next open, until Android stops asking. After that, heart rate can only come back through system settings, and the watch never says so.
- **Why it matters:** The first thing the watch app does is show system dialogs whose purpose, live heart rate during sets, can't be seen yet. That makes a denial likely, and soon permanent.
- **Proposal:** Ask when the answer matters: the first time a session is live while the activity is in front and the heart-rate permission is missing. Show one watch screen: a mono "HEART RATE", one body line "Live heart rate during your sets", a filled "Allow" button, and swipe to skip. Then launch the request. Store the skip for the install, so the app never asks again on its own. If HR is denied, its line stays hidden. Asking while the activity is in front also means a grant can start HR straight away (`applySession` re-runs on grant, [MainActivity.kt:30](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/MainActivity.kt#L30)). That is the while-in-use path the 2026-09-12 audit's W04 calls for.
- **Doctrine:** SETTLED: "nothing should interrupt a cold launch to ask for a permission" ([SETTLED.md:91](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L91)). WEAR.md: one decision per screen. DESIGN §12: a signal whose permission was never granted is hidden.
