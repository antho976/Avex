# Stats, session history and session detail

Scope: the Stats tab (`ui/gym/stats/` with `components/` and `state/`), History (`ui/gym/history/`) and session detail (`ui/gym/session/`), plus the routes into them and the data they read. 18 raised, 18 kept (13 revised), 0 dropped. Six parts of kept findings were cut; they are listed at the end.

The screens are well made at the surface: open sections, honest-zero figures, one shared History row, draw-in motion. The debt is depth and reach. Every list and chart stops one level short of the question a lifter brings to it. Lifts are capped at six with no overflow, charts carry no dates and their points can't be opened, no page shows one lift's sessions, and search finds sessions without showing the sets that matched. Inside session detail, PRs, deltas and the muscle map each use a different idiom from the rest of the app. Two routes are missing altogether: a finished gym workout can't be removed, and the month and year Recap has no way in.

## Findings

### STH-02 · Strength and Records stop at six lifts, and inert Records rows look tappable
missing-state · impact high · effort M · Stats > Strength lens

- **Now:** `buildE1rmLifts` sorts by current e1RM and keeps six ([StatsStrengthAggregations.kt:137-138](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsStrengthAggregations.kt#L137-L138)). Neither list has an overflow, so curls, raises, rows and most accessories never appear on Stats, and the heaviest machine and leg lifts take every slot. Every Records row is a member of one filled `surfaceContainerHigh` group ([StatsOverview.kt:143-153](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/StatsOverview.kt#L143-L153)). Only rows in `drillableIds` (top-six e1RM lifts with 2+ sessions, [StatsContent.kt:230-234](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/StatsContent.kt#L230-L234)) are clickable. A record for the seventh lift, or for a one-session lift, looks identical and does nothing.
- **Why it matters:** Anyone who trains more than six movements can't see most of their lifts' progress or records. The prior audit's P2 fix stopped the no-op tap but kept the pressable look, so the inert rows still read as broken.
- **Proposal:** Keep both six-row trims. Under each, add `view all N →` (N = lifts with a weighted set) leading to a lift index. The index is a List archetype screen: search field first, then one trim row per lift led by its `ExerciseIcons.forEquipment` glyph, with current e1RM as the right figure and the last-trained date in the meta. Each row opens the lift page (STH-01), so this ships with STH-01 or after it. Every Records row then routes to the lift page, and `drillableIds` goes away. If STH-01 isn't scheduled, use an interim fix: build an `E1rmLift` entry for every Records lift (the prior audit's first option), so every row expands in place, and draw any row that still can't act without the slab. Revised: the dependency on STH-01 and the interim fix are new.
- **Doctrine:** §12 overflow is a trim plus `view all →`, never "show more" ([DESIGN.md:306](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L306)). §3 List: search first, trim rows ([DESIGN.md:86](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L86)). §8: exercise rows in browsers lead with the equipment glyph ([DESIGN.md:230](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L230)). ### STH-03 · Charts can't be read against time: no dates, no point to open, deloads computed but not drawn
feature-gap · impact high · effort M · Stats > Strength row trend, Volume > Weekly volume

- **Now:** `E1rmLift.history` is a bare `List<Double>` with no dates and no session ids ([StatsUiState.kt:117-130](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/state/StatsUiState.kt#L117-L130)). The e1RM trend labels only its low and high values, and its x axis is the session index ([StatsStrength.kt:225-247](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/StatsStrength.kt#L225-L247)). So a 14-point line can't say when it started or which session the peak was. The RECENT PRS rows under it are passive text ([StatsStrength.kt:255-266](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/StatsStrength.kt#L255-L266)). Weekly volume plots `WeeklyTonnage`, which carries `weekLabel` (a raw "MM-dd" built at [StatsVolumeAggregations.kt:86](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/StatsVolumeAggregations.kt#L86)) and `isDeload` ([StatsUiState.kt:54-58](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/state/StatsUiState.kt#L54-L58)). The chart reads neither ([StatsVolume.kt:179-203](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/StatsVolume.kt#L179-L203)), although its own KDoc says "Deload weeks dip by design" ([StatsVolume.kt:174-176](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/StatsVolume.kt#L174-L176)). `LineChart` takes values only and has no touch handling ([LineChart.kt:14-35](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/components/LineChart.kt#L14-L35)).
- **Why it matters:** Stats can't answer "when did my squat stall?" or "was that dip a deload?", and no point on a chart can be traced to the session it came from.
- **Proposal:** Carry `(date, sessionId, value)` per point. Add `le.session_id` to the `SetWithExerciseAndSession` query, and keep each point's `sessionStartedAt` in `buildE1rmLifts`. Give `WeeklyTonnage` a `LocalDate` week start and translate it at render ("Week of Sep 15") instead of storing "MM-dd". Then:
  1. Under each chart, print the first and last dates as mono labelS captions beside the low and high values.
  2. Add press-and-drag scrub to `LineChart`. It draws a vertical data hairline (outline 0.25) at the nearest point and a mono readout line above the chart ("SEP 24 · 112.5 KG", "WEEK OF SEP 15 · 14.2K KG · DELOAD").
  3. A tap on the readout opens that session's detail. Releasing the drag does nothing, so scrubbing can never navigate by accident.
  4. Draw deload weeks as hollow rings on the tonnage line.
  5. Make each RECENT PRS row a whole-row tap into its session (`PrEntry` gains `sessionId`).

  The readout string can also serve as the chart's `contentDescription`. Revised: navigation moved from drag release to a tap on the readout, and the stored week label is translated at render.
- **Doctrine:** §4.5: chart points answer a tap with detail ([DESIGN.md:99](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L99)). §4.2: data drill-downs are navigation in place ([DESIGN.md:96](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L96)). §1: the scrub hairline is a line that is data. §11 translates the machine week label ([DESIGN.md:278](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L278)). FAILURES *Invisible ghost*: the deload is marked by shape, ring against disc, not by luminance ([FAILURES.md:73](../../../.claude/design/FAILURES.md#L73)). There is no floating tooltip box, so no boxed passive content. This complements, and does not repeat, the prior audit's P3 on chart semantics ([02-gym-freestyle-stats-history.md](../2026-09-26/02-gym-freestyle-stats-history.md)).

### STH-07 · Strength bars rank every lift against your strongest one
ui-polish · impact medium · effort S · Stats > Strength per lift

- **Now:** Each bar is `currentE1rm / max(currentE1rm)` across the lifts ([StatsStrength.kt:81-89](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/StatsStrength.kt#L81-L89)), under the caption "Estimated 1RM, ranked" ([StatsContent.kt:214](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/stats/StatsContent.kt#L214)).
