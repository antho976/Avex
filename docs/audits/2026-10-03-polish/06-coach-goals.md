# Coach tab and goals

Scope: the Coach tab (`ui/coach/`: the ledger, open call tiles, Where you stand, Block, Learned, the advanced-tracking offer) and Goals (`ui/goals/`: the ranked list and the goal editor), plus the repositories they write through. 17 raised, 15 kept (7 revised), 2 dropped.

The ledger's shape is finished. Its debt is in what each entry carries and what it lets you take back. Consent is lopsided: Apply has an Undo on the entry, but Skip, End the block and Clear goal are one tap with no way back. The engine also computes readings that the UI then throws away: the watcher's reason for a verdict, the week's focus cue, a lift's move in numbers on the call, and whether autopilot applied a change. As a result the record states verdicts with nothing behind them, and stops at six weeks. Goals are the weakest seam. The block plans from a goal table no screen can write, and a lift target is set without seeing your own numbers.

## Findings

### CCH-01 · Skip is one tap, can't be taken back, and costs the change type its autopilot streak
ux-friction · impact high · effort S · Coach > THIS WEEK > open call tile

- **Now:** Skip is a muted `CoachAction` beside the full-width Apply ([CoachCall.kt:127-133](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachCall.kt#L127-L133)). `skipDecision` flips proposed to skipped, and nothing reverses it ([CoachRepository.kt:763-766](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/CoachRepository.kt#L763-L766), [CoachViewModel.kt:239](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachViewModel.kt#L239)). `DecisionEntry` draws Undo only for `STATUS_APPLIED` ([CoachAccount.kt:336-344](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachAccount.kt#L336-L344)), so a skipped call becomes a bare `SKIPPED` line (`DB Curl · Move to 12 reps` in [coach-ledger-basic.png](../../../forge-android/app/src/test/screenshots/coach-ledger-basic.png)). The skip also breaks that type's accepted streak toward autopilot ([TrustLedger.kt:79-89](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/coach/TrustLedger.kt#L79-L89)). For a swap or a rep-range shift, it is recorded as a decline, and the planner never proposes that same change again ([CoachRepository.kt:518-524](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/CoachRepository.kt#L518-L524)).
- **Why it matters:** One mis-tap during the Monday ritual throws away the week's change, resets that type's progress toward autopilot, and, for a rotation, permanently blocks that same rotation from coming back. PRODUCT promises "apply and skip are one tap, undo stays available" ([PRODUCT.md:40](../../../PRODUCT.md#L40)) and "consent is always reversible" ([PRODUCT.md:121](../../../PRODUCT.md#L121)). Only half of that holds today.
- **Proposal:** Add `CoachRepository.reopenDecision(id)`, run under `lifecycleMutex`. It moves skipped back to proposed only when the decision's `weekId` is the latest pass. Older weeks must stay closed, because `expireProposalsBefore` also writes `skipped` ([CoachDao.kt:123-124](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/db/dao/CoachDao.kt#L123-L124)). On a skipped entry in the current week, `DecisionEntry` draws the same `ForgeRowPill("Undo")` that applied entries carry. The whole entry is the target, through `clickableLabeled("Undo skip, <subject>")`. The tap brings the open tile back in place. The trust streak and the decline memory are both recomputed from the ledger, so both recover with no special handling. No snackbar is needed: Apply's Undo lives on the entry, and Skip should match it. Revised: the finder proposed both a snackbar and a new verb, "Reopen". This keeps one mechanism and one verb, and adds the decline-memory consequence.
- **Doctrine:** §12 undo over confirm, no dialog ([DESIGN.md:323](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L323)). §8 per-row action is a drawn outlined pill, reusing the existing Undo pattern, so there is no second filled capsule (FAILURES *Button wall*) and no nested tap ([FAILURES.md:176](../../../.claude/design/FAILURES.md#L176)). SETTLED keeps "apply / skip / undo on every change" as non-negotiable ([SETTLED.md:132](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L132)). This is not tracked in audits 07 or 10, or in AUDIT_DEFERRED.
- **Related bug (out of scope, for the coach-engine tracker):** `expireProposalsBefore` stamps un-tapped proposals from earlier weeks `skipped`. The record then says `SKIPPED` for calls the user never touched, and TrustLedger counts them as skips.

### CCH-02 · The evidence chart on a call shows a shape, not a reading

- **Now:** `CallEvidence` draws `ESTIMATED 1RM · LAST N SESSIONS` over an 88dp sparkline with no axis, no endpoint values and no delta ([CoachCall.kt:193-209](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachCall.kt#L193-L209)). Its `contentDescription` names no value. In [coach-ledger.png](../../../forge-android/app/src/test/screenshots/coach-ledger.png) the DB Row rotation's curve wobbles, and nothing says by how much. - **Why it matters:** The call is the one thing the user approves from, and default users get no number to check. PRODUCT must-survive #2 ("the evidence ... visible before the user approves", [PRODUCT.md:88](../../../PRODUCT.md#L88)) is only half met. A stall call reads the same as a rising trend until you study the curve.
- **Proposal:** The caption becomes the reading: `ESTIMATED 1RM · 212 → 231 LB · ↑ 9% OVER 9 SESSIONS`. Build it from `formatWeight(series.first()/last(), weightUnit)` and `liftTrendWord`. It is fed the `TrackedLift` found in `state.watch.trackedLifts` by `slotId == d.targetKey`, and falls back to the raw percentage when the slot isn't tracked. Use the same string as the sparkline's `contentDescription`. Under advanced tracking, `Lifts on watch` skips lifts that have an open call, because their evidence now sits on the call.
- **Doctrine:** FAILURES *Verdict without a reading* ([FAILURES.md:122](../../../.claude/design/FAILURES.md#L122)) and §4.9 ([DESIGN.md:103](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L103)). The new caption replaces the old one, so there is no caption stack. `coachStand`'s own KDoc and MAP say "the evidence for any specific CALL lives on that call", and skipping those lifts in Signals keeps one home (§4.3). Audit 07 P4 lists this sparkline under "charts without value descriptions" ([07:112](../2026-09-26/07-coach-onboarding-builder-experiment.md)). This finding closes that line for `CoachCall` as a side effect. The visible reading is the new part.

- **Now:** `BlockRepository.start` takes its focus and intent from `coachGoalRepository.active()` ([BlockRepository.kt:69-83](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/BlockRepository.kt#L69-L83)). The directive's weekly target reads a CONSISTENCY `CoachGoal` ([DirectiveRepository.kt:124-131](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/DirectiveRepository.kt#L124-L131)). The only UI that writes that table is `GoalPickerDialog`, through `CoachViewModel.addGoal`, and nothing renders the dialog ([CoachScreen.kt:189-195](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachScreen.kt#L189-L195)). Import and backup restore are the only other writers. Goals set on the Goals screen (`exercise_goal`, `extended_goal`) feed none of this. So every block started from the app gets the intent "Build steadily, then take a planned week back.", and the Peak test-week line says "take one heavy top set on your focus lift" without naming a lift ([CoachBlock.kt:59-74](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachBlock.kt#L59-L74)). Coach has no way into Goals.
- **Why it matters:** A lifter who set "Bench Press 120 kg" starts a block that builds toward nothing named. PRODUCT's longer-arc layer (goals + block, [PRODUCT.md:91](../../../PRODUCT.md#L91)) isn't connected to itself.
- **Proposal:**
  1. When no `CoachGoal` is active, `BlockRepository.start` uses the user's top live Goals-screen goal. It takes the first pinned goal, or else the closest one, which is the order the Goals screen already ranks by. The mapping:
     - a lift target becomes `LIFT_1RM` on that lift
     - `SESSIONS`/`WEEK` becomes `CONSISTENCY`
     - `BODYWEIGHT` stays `BODYWEIGHT`
     - the cardio metrics become `CONDITIONING`

     The intent names the goal ("Build toward Bench Press 120 kg, then test it."), and so does the test-week line. `DirectiveRepository` reads a `SESSIONS`/`WEEK` goal's target the same way.
  2. The BLOCK figure caption names that goal with its live reading. Beside it, a `goals →` `CoachAction` in onBg opens `Routes.GOALS`, which gives Coach a door to the screen it plans from.
  3. Record in SETTLED that this settles the "two parallel goal systems" question. Deleting the dead `CoachGoal` UI path is already audit 07's P3 ([07:83](../2026-09-26/07-coach-onboarding-builder-experiment.md)).

  Revised: the finder's retirement of the CoachGoal UI is dropped as a duplicate, and the hidden-Home-goals reachability claim is dropped (it is the user's own choice, and Settings → Appearance restores it).
- **Doctrine:** SETTLED leaves the two goal systems as "the open question ... worth resolving before either grows again" ([SETTLED.md:566](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L566)). This answers it, so it needs Antho's sign-off before it is built. There is no per-goal archive on Coach (SETTLED removal, [SETTLED.md:129](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L129)) and no Academy link (SETTLED, [SETTLED.md:94](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L94)). Coach gets no copy of Home's `GoalProgressLine`, so there is no mark echo (§4.3). The link sits beside the goal's own reading (§4.2), in onBg (§14). AUDIT_DEFERRED H-02 keeps Peak's test-week *prescription* open. Naming the lift is copy only, and the prescription stays tracked there.

### CCH-14 · Setting a lift target is blind: no current best, and the whole library to scroll
ux-friction · impact medium · effort M · Goals > Add a goal > Lift target (picker, then target form)

- **Now:** With an empty query, `LiftPickerStep` lists the whole eligible library in library order ([GoalEditorScreen.kt:297-336](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/goals/GoalEditorScreen.kt#L297-L336), [ExerciseLibraryPicker.kt:50-56](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/ExerciseLibraryPicker.kt#L50-L56)). Nothing shows which lifts you train or what you lift. `LiftWeightStep` then asks for "Target" with placeholder "0" and no reading ([GoalEditorScreen.kt:380-429](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/goals/GoalEditorScreen.kt#L380-L429)). Progress is measured against the all-time heaviest set ([GoalRepository.kt:59-77](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/GoalRepository.kt#L59-L77)). So a target typed at or below a weight already lifted lands in Reached the moment it is saved.
- **Why it matters:** Anyone adding a lift goal has to hunt for their own bench in a generic list and guess a number from memory. A low or mistyped target is "reached" on save.
- **Proposal:** `GoalsViewModel` exposes the heaviest set per logged exercise (`loggedSetDao.maxWeightPerExercise`, the query `goalsWithProgress` already runs), plus the active program's lift ids. With an empty query, the picker leads with the program's lifts under a `ForgeGroupLabel("Your lifts")`, each with its heaviest set as the right meta, and then the library. `LiftWeightStep` gets one `ForgeGroupCaption` under the field: "Heaviest so far 105 kg". When the typed target is at or below that, it reads "Already lifted. Aim above 105 kg". Weights go through `formatWeight` and `weightUnit`.
- **Doctrine:** §4.9 show the reading. The List archetype stays search-first, with the search row unchanged on top. §13: the explainer is at most one line, beside the field. Each row carries its own reading, so this is not a checklist (§4.10). Picker rows keep their equipment glyph (§8). Audit 06's goals nits cover only the picker query surviving rotation and its duplication of `ExerciseLibraryPicker`.

### CCH-12 · "End the block →" throws away the block's position in one tap, with no Undo
ux-friction · impact low · effort S · Coach > BLOCK (advanced)

- **Now:** The action sits alone under the phase rail ([CoachBlock.kt:77-87](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachBlock.kt#L77-L87)). `endBlock` calls `BlockRepository.end`, which stamps `endedAt` on every open row, with no snackbar and no reverse ([CoachViewModel.kt:329-338](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachViewModel.kt#L329-L338), [BlockRepository.kt:186-193](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/BlockRepository.kt#L186-L193)). Starting again inserts a fresh block at Week 1 ([BlockRepository.kt:73-100](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/BlockRepository.kt#L73-L100)).
- **Why it matters:** An accidental end in week 5 of 6 undoes weeks of periodisation, and nothing brings it back.
- **Proposal:** `endBlock` captures the active block's id, then calls `snackbar.showUndo("Block ended") { blockRepo.resume(id) }`. `resume` clears `endedAt` in the same transaction pattern `start` uses, but only if no other block has gone live in the meantime.
- **Doctrine:** §12 undo over confirm via `SnackbarController`, with no dialog. §8: a destructive one-shot is paired with Undo. Nothing is added to the page. Audit 07 P4 ("`endBlock` clears the block even when `end()` failed") is the correctness half and stays there.

### CCH-16 · A custom goal can't be renamed or moved to a different timeframe
ux-friction · impact low · effort S · Goals > edit custom goal

- **Now:** `CustomEditStep` offers only Target and Show on Home ([GoalEditorScreen.kt:515-561](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/goals/GoalEditorScreen.kt#L515-L561)). Name and Timeframe are set once, in `CustomNewStep` ([GoalEditorScreen.kt:437-511](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/goals/GoalEditorScreen.kt#L437-L511)). The repository exposes only create, updateTarget, delete and restore ([ExtendedGoalRepository.kt:64-97](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/data/repo/ExtendedGoalRepository.kt#L64-L97)).
- **Why it matters:** A typo in a goal's name is permanent. Turning a weekly goal into a monthly one means deleting and recreating it. A bodyweight goal recreated just to rename it takes a new weigh-in baseline, so its bar changes meaning.
- **Proposal:** `CustomEditStep` reuses `CustomNewStep`'s Timeframe `ForgeSlidingSegments` row (cumulative metrics only) and its Name `ForgeFieldRow`, in the same `ForgeRowGroup`. Add `ExtendedGoalRepository.update(id, period, target, label)`, which writes only the fields that changed and keeps the existing `storedUnlessEdited` rule for the target. The `goal_type` encoding is unchanged.
- **Doctrine:** Editor archetype: real controls from the grouped-surface kit (§8), nothing invented, no dialog. The SETTLED goal-ladder decisions are untouched.

### CCH-17 · TalkBack hears "Apply", "Skip this change", "Undo this change", with no subject
accessibility · impact low · effort S · Coach > call tiles and account entries

- **Now:** Apply is a `ForgePrimaryCapsule`, whose only accessible name is its visible label ([CoachCall.kt:127-131](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachCall.kt#L127-L131), [Capsules.kt:31-62](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/common/Capsules.kt#L31-L62)). Skip announces "Skip this change" ([CoachCall.kt:133](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachCall.kt#L133)), and every Undo pill announces "Undo this change" ([CoachAccount.kt:340-343](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/coach/CoachAccount.kt#L340-L343)). With two open calls, moving control to control reads "Apply, Skip this change, Apply, Skip this change", with nothing saying which change.
- **Why it matters:** A TalkBack user who jumps between controls can't tell which change a control acts on without stepping back through the tile. Until CCH-01 lands, that matters most for Skip, which can't be undone.
- **Proposal:** Add an optional `contentDescription` to `ForgePrimaryCapsule`, as a semantics override that leaves the visible label unchanged. Build the labels from `callCopy(d)`, for example "Apply Bench Press, 3 to 4 sets". Pass the same pattern into the descriptions that `CoachAction` and `clickableLabeled` already take for Skip and Undo, and say "to" for the arrow. Revised: impact lowered from medium to low, because swiping in order reads the tile's subject before its controls. The ambiguity only appears when jumping control to control.
- **Doctrine:** §14 semantics ([DESIGN.md:367](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L367)) and PRODUCT's TalkBack requirement. No visual change, and the capsule stays the one shared component, with no fork. Audit 07 covers chart descriptions and touch targets, not control labels.
