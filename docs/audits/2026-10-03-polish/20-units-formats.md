# Unit and format switching across the app (lb / kg / st, km / mi, cm / in, 12h / 24h)

Scope: Settings → Units & format and Equipment → Loading; the live set input, set rows and exercise card; freestyle; the watch set view; the coach's load grids; bodyweight entry (weigh-in, check-in, photo viewer, goals, Profile BODY); cardio distance; session detail; the Academy (`domain/units/`, `ui/settings/`, `ui/gym/train/components/`, `ui/gym/freestyle/`, `domain/adapt/`, `domain/coach/`, `ui/profile/`, `ui/checkin/`, `ui/goals/`, `ui/cardio/components/`, `ui/academy/`, wear `SessionScreen.kt`). 12 raised, 12 kept (10 revised), 0 dropped. Parts cut from kept findings are listed in the dropped table.

The formatters hold up. Typed suffixes, comma decimals and untouched seeds all round-trip, and the 2026-09-26 unit bugs are fixed. The debt sits around them. Each surface picked its own load grid: the coach uses 2.5 lb, the stepper 5 lb, the warm-up 5 or 2.5 lb, and the plate settings use pound hardware converted to kg. So the numbers the app offers often can't be loaded, and the stepper can't reach them. One Weight setting covers both the bar and the scale, which makes stones a lifting unit and leaves stones bodyweight written two ways. Quantities that don't fit km or lb, like swims and kg plates, never got a unit of their own. Every fix below changes behaviour or copy; none adds a screen.

## Findings

### UNT-02 · The live weight field opens the letters keyboard
ux-friction · impact high · effort S · Live session → set input row → WEIGHT field

- **Now:** The weight `BigNumberField` asks for `KeyboardType.Text` ([SetInputRow.kt:395](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L395)) and filters nothing ([SetInputRow.kt:198-213](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L198-L213)). The legacy compact branch does the same ([SetInputRow.kt:619](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L619)). Every field around it is numeric. Reps, beside it, is `KeyboardType.Number` ([SetInputRow.kt:316](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L316)). The inline edit of the same set is `Decimal` ([SetRow.kt:204](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetRow.kt#L204)). Freestyle's weight field is `Decimal` and runs through `filterDecimalInput` ([FreestyleLogParts.kt:505-508](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/freestyle/FreestyleLogParts.kt#L505-L508)). The letters keyboard only serves shortcuts the screen never mentions: "45x" jumps to reps, and `parseToLb` accepts a typed unit ("20 kg", "9 st 4") ([WeightFormatter.kt:163](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/units/WeightFormatter.kt#L163)).
- **Why it matters:** The weight gets typed on every first-time exercise and whenever the seed is wrong. On most keyboards that means switching to the symbols layer between sets, with an autocorrect strip above the keys.
- **Proposal:** Switch both weight fields to `KeyboardType.Decimal` and keep `ImeAction.Next`. Next already moves focus to reps, which covers what the "x" shortcut did. In `onWeightChange`, match `WEIGHT_REPS_REGEX` against the raw text first, so a pasted "45x10" still splits, then pass everything else through `filterDecimalInput` as freestyle does. Plate counts get the same keyboard. Note the trade-off for review: the stones compound and unit suffixes can no longer be typed here. Nothing on screen advertises them, and UNT-01 takes stones out of lifting. The other half, a typo like "6O" logging a weightless set, is the 2026-09-26 P3 in [01-gym-train.md](../2026-09-26/01-gym-train.md) ("require the text to parse") and stays there.
- **Doctrine:** §13: hot-path numbers are steppers plus inline edit, and when a keyboard is needed it is the numeric one, as on reps and SetRow's edit ([DESIGN.md:328](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L328)). No element is added. SETTLED lists the live-session screen as untouchable ([SETTLED.md:138](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L138)), so this is a behaviour-only change that needs Antho's sign-off like every live-screen item. It brings the live screen in line with freestyle and adds no new pattern.
  Revised: the regex now runs before the filter (the finder's order would have turned "45x10" into "4510"), and the weightless-set bug is left with its existing audit entry.

### UNT-03 · Off-grid weights stay off-grid: the ± pill adds 2.5 to 61.2
ux-friction · impact medium · effort S · Live session ± pill, freestyle stepper, watch bezel

- **Now:** History is stored in lb, so after an lb→kg switch the seed is a converted pound load: 135 lb seeds "61.2" ([SetInputRow.kt:132-134](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L132-L134)). `stepWeight` adds the step to whatever is in the field ([SetInputRow.kt:260-266](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/train/components/SetInputRow.kt#L260-L266)), so the pill gives 63.7, 66.2 or 58.7. Freestyle's `stepWeightText` does the same, using its own copy of the step table, `weightStepFor` ([FreestyleLogModel.kt:163-181](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/gym/freestyle/FreestyleLogModel.kt#L163-L181)). So does the watch's step lambda ([SessionScreen.kt:131-140](../../../forge-android/wear/src/main/java/com/forge/wear/ui/SessionScreen.kt#L131-L140)). Stones history (135 lb seeds "9.6", step 0.5), imported histories, and a typed 47.5 lb (UNT-04) hit the same thing.
- **Why it matters:** For anyone whose history isn't on today's grid, the first + makes the number worse rather than better, so every set has to be typed.
- **Proposal:** Add `WeightSteps.snap(value, step, up)` in :shared. + goes to the next multiple of the step strictly above the value, and − to the next one strictly below, with a small epsilon so values already on the grid move exactly one step. Call it from `SetInputRow.stepWeight`, from freestyle's `stepWeightText`, and once per detent in the watch's `step` lambda. Retire `weightStepFor` in favour of `WeightSteps.weightStep`, so the phone and the wrist keep sharing one table, as the WeightSteps KDoc requires ([WeightSteps.kt:20-35](../../../forge-android/shared/src/main/kotlin/com/forge/shared/weight/WeightSteps.kt#L20-L35)). Leave seeds and the "keep this weight" cue alone: both return a lifted weight verbatim on purpose ([ProgressionAdvisor.kt:192-198](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/adapt/ProgressionAdvisor.kt#L192-L198), [PreSessionBrief.kt:130-134](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/coach/PreSessionBrief.kt#L130-L134)).
- **Doctrine:** §13: steppers are the hot-path input, and this makes them land on real loads. No element is added, and SETTLED's untouchable live screen keeps its shape (behaviour only; flag for Antho). PRODUCT principle 1 holds, because only a user tap moves a number. It complements TRL-01 and TRL-02 in [02-train-logging.md](02-train-logging.md), which change which value seeds, not its grid.
  Revised: seed snapping is dropped (see the table). Only the stepper snaps.

### UNT-05 · Equipment → Loading offers kg users converted pound plates
consistency · impact medium · effort S · Settings → Program & equipment → Equipment → Loading, and the Equipment row's value

- **Now:** Both chip rows are fixed lb lists, [5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 45] and [15 … 100], printed through `plateHardwareLabel` ([SettingsProgramPage.kt:356-367](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/settings/SettingsProgramPage.kt#L356-L367)). A kg user is offered plates of "2.3 kg · 4.5 kg · 6.8 kg · 9.1 kg · 11.3 kg · 20.4 kg" and dumbbells from "6.8 kg" to "45.4 kg". A chip lights only on exact equality (`state.plateWeightLb == w`, [:359](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/settings/SettingsProgramPage.kt#L359)). Onboarding's closing step offers real kg plates, 1.25 to 25 kg ([OnboardingExtras.kt:211-213](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/onboarding/OnboardingExtras.kt#L211-L213)), and a metric locale defaults to 10 kg ([OnboardingScreen.kt:206-209](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/onboarding/OnboardingScreen.kt#L206-L209)). A kg user who kept that default reads "10 kg plates" in the Equipment row ([SettingsProgramPage.kt:155-161](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/ui/settings/SettingsProgramPage.kt#L155-L161)), finds no chip lit on the page, and can't get 10 kg back once they tap another chip.
- **Why it matters:** kg lifters can't describe their own plates or rack. If they pick the nearest chip, every plate-loaded set is then counted with a 9.1 kg plate, about 9% light, and the dumbbell ceiling is a converted pound weight.
- **Proposal:** Move the denominations into one table in `domain/units`, for example `PlateHardware.plates(unit)` and `.dumbbellCeilings(unit)`. kg plates: 1.25, 2.5, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25. kg dumbbells: 10, 12.5, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50. The lb lists stay as they are, and stones keeps using lb hardware as `plateHardwareLabel` already does. Store values through `fromDisplayWeight` and select with the 0.05 lb tolerance onboarding already uses. If the stored value matches no chip (it came from the other unit), draw it as one extra lit chip, so the live value is always on the page. Onboarding's `TrainingGroup` reads the same table.
- **Doctrine:** §3 Settings: real controls that show the live value ([DESIGN.md:87](../../../.claude/DESIGN.md#L87)), which kg users don't get today. §11: nothing hardcoded to one unit. `LoadingChips` and `SettingsFilterChip` are reused, with no new control or page. SEP-02 in [09-settings-prefs.md](09-settings-prefs.md) (Generate copy) is a separate issue.

### UNT-04 · The coach's lb grid is 2.5 lb, so it asks for a 47.5 lb dumbbell the + pill can't reach
consistency · impact medium · effort M · Live session coach cue ("try … lb") vs the ± pill, the warm-up ramp and the Academy warm-up calculator

- **Now:** For lb, `loadStepLb` returns `dumbbellStepLb` = 2.5 for every exercise type ([AdaptThresholds.kt:16-17](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/adapt/AdaptThresholds.kt#L16-L17), [:228-232](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/adapt/AdaptThresholds.kt#L228-L232)). So 45 lb dumbbells at the top of the range become "try 47.5 lb", and a 135 lb bench becomes "try 137.5 lb", which needs 1.25 lb plates. The ± pill steps 5 lb ([WeightSteps.kt:30-35](../../../forge-android/shared/src/main/kotlin/com/forge/shared/weight/WeightSteps.kt#L30-L35)), so from 45 it jumps to 50 and never lands on the cue. The other lb surfaces already use 5. `WarmupEngine.loadIncrement` uses 5 lb for dumbbells and 2.5 lb for barbells ([WarmupEngine.kt:172-177](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/warmup/WarmupEngine.kt#L172-L177)). - **Why it matters:** For an lb user, every dumbbell progression names a dumbbell most racks don't have. Stepping to the 50 they actually own counts as a heavier override, because anything more than 0.1 lb above the cue does ([SuggestionCalibrator.kt:52](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/coach/SuggestionCalibrator.kt#L52), [:83-86](../../../forge-android/app/src/main/java/com/forge/app/domain/coach/SuggestionCalibrator.kt#L83-L86)). The calibrator is reading the rack's spacing as the lifter's strength.
- **Proposal:** 1. Derive `AdaptThresholds.loadStepLb(LB)` from `WeightSteps.weightStep(LB, false)`, which is 5 lb, as the 2026-09-26 engine fix proposed ("5 lb or 2.5 lb dumbbells for lb", [10-coach-adapt-engine-program.md:16](../2026-09-26/10-coach-adapt-engine-program.md)). Set `WarmupEngine.loadIncrement` for an lb barbell to 5 too. The cue, the pill, the ramp and the brief then share one table. Update the ProgressionAdvisor tests that pin 2.5 lb. 2. Optional, as a separate change: add a "Smallest jump" chip row (Standard | Fractional plates) to Equipment → Loading for lifters who own microplates. It halves the barbell and machine step (2.5 lb or 1.25 kg) for both the advisor and the pill. Without it, they lose the 2.5 lb barbell jumps the coach offers today.
- **Doctrine:** §11: numbers in the user's own terms. PRODUCT principle 1: the coach still proposes each set, and only its grid changes. The optional chips go in the existing Loading group and show their live value. That is a control the app reads, so SETTLED 2026-09-26's "a control that changes nothing" doesn't apply ([SETTLED.md:27-37](../../../.claude/design/SETTLED.md#L27-L37)). The kg half of the 2026-09-26 P1 shipped and the lb half did not, and this covers the lb half. It is a grid-consistency change, not a correctness bug: 47.5 is a legal weight, just not one the rack or the stepper offers.
  Revised: the calibrator cost replaces the finder's unconfirmed "override" claim, part 1 reuses the existing WeightSteps table instead of adding a new `LoadGrid` type, and the chips are split out as optional.

Lifting keeps `weightUnit`, now only lb or kg, and the watch protocol stops receiving ST. Migrate an existing st user to bodyweight st, and pick lifts from their own data: kg when `plateWeightLb` is within 0.05 lb of a kg plate (a metric-locale onboarding stores 10 kg), lb otherwise. Add the new key to backup and to the section reset. Edit DESIGN §11 so the tri-state belongs to bodyweight, and record the decision in DECISIONS. UNT-06 then applies to the bodyweight unit.

  Revised: impact lowered to medium because stones is opt-in from Settings only. The finder migrated st users to lifts in lb, copying the plate-chip fallback; this version derives lifts from the user's stored plate weight instead.
