# Baseline, 2026-11-18

The first timed reading of Avex. Everything here is from the device, not from reading code.

- **Device:** Galaxy S21 Ultra (SM-G998U), Android 15, 720×1600 display mode, on USB power.
- **Build:** `perf` (release, R8, the shipped baseline profile, `speed-profile` at install), at
  commit `83119ca6`: main plus the timing marks and nothing else.
- **Data:** onboarded, generated 4-day plan, eight weeks of seeded sessions.
- **Harness:** `forge-android/tools/perf/coldstart.py`; screen recordings read frame by frame.

## Cold start

16 cold starts, interleaved with the fixed build on the same installed data (see FIXES). Times are
ms after the process starts, except the two `system.*` rows, which are from the launch intent
(about 85 ms earlier).

| Milestone | p50 | p95 | max |
|---|---|---|---|
| First frame, `system.displayed` | 206 | 221 | 223 |
| Home composed | 122 | 132 | 136 |
| Home's week, recents and goals in (`home.state`) | 186 | 202 | 209 |
| Home's two readings in (`home.slots`) | 202 | 217 | 223 |
| Today's directive in | 252 | 268 | 275 |
| Coach row in | 295 | 305 | 313 |
| **Home fully loaded** | **295** | **305** | **313** |
| Fully drawn, `system.fullyDrawn` | 359 | 372 | 381 |
| Launch settled (splash gone) | 438 | 449 | 453 |

**On screen** (5 recordings, tap to the last visible change): 461, 498, 524, 524, 933 ms; median
**524 ms**.

The installed 0.9 release on the same phone, before any of this: first frame 200 ms median over
10 cold starts (186 to 216), and about 0.69 s on screen in one recording.

### What a launch looked like

`raw/launch-before-after-2026-11-18.png`, top row. The splash went at about 250 ms and Home faded
in while it was still loading:

- at 260 ms it read "Upper A" with a filled "Start session" and "0 min";
- at 340 ms "Upper B";
- at 380 ms "Done for today" and an outlined "Train anyway", the real answer, with no coach row;
- at 460 ms the coach row arrived and pushed the week strip and the button down;
- the page was still rising into place (a 450 ms entrance) until about 520 ms.

The headline and the main button changed twice under the thumb in the first 200 ms.

### Where the time went

| Cost | ms | What it was |
|---|---|---|
| Refresh debounce | 100 | `ConflatedRefresh` waited 100 ms before every read. The directive, the coach row and the body readings were asked for at 131 ms and did not start until 250 ms. The directive itself took 4 ms. |
| Reads started late | ~30 | Home's reads started only after the screen had composed and its lifecycle reported the resume, then queued behind that frame on the main thread. |
| Placeholder frame | ~20 | The Activity drew "Preparing Avex…" first, hopped to a background thread for settings that were already in memory, and composed Home a frame later. |
| Hand-off | 240 + overlap | Splash fade 240 ms, over a nav root fading in for 320 ms and Home rising for 450 ms. |

Not a cost: the baseline profile. The shipped profile is two wildcard rules, and R8 expands them
to 24,967 rules covering the app's own classes, so the app's code is compiled ahead of time at
install (`dumpsys package dexopt`: `speed-profile`).

One recording each, tap to the last visible change, on the fixed build (these paths were not

Every one is the length of its transition: the tab slide and the push both run on the Emphasized
token, 320 ms. The screen's content is there when the transition lands. Coach's first open is the
one that runs on after it (about 100 ms).

## Not measured

- Opening a workout day, logging a set, finishing a session (they change the data they are
  measured on; they need a seeded scenario of their own).
- Scroll smoothness (frame timing) on History, Stats and the exercise picker.
- A history of years rather than eight weeks, and a slower phone. The coach snapshot reads the
  whole history, so its share grows with it.
- Health Connect connected. On this phone nothing is granted, so the body readings answer at once.
- Battery and memory.
