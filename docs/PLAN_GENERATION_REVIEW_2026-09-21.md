# Plan generation review: selection quality, overlap and ordering

Reviewed 2026-09-21 on `claude/plan-making-system-review-46wxow`. Scope: `ProgramGenerator`, `SplitTemplates`, `VolumeModel`, `GoalProfiles`, `ExerciseLibrary` metadata (patterns, tags, pick bias, contraindications), the regenerate / re-roll / deload paths in `ProgramRepository`, and the coach's stall-swap proposal. The question asked was: what produces a suboptimal plan, the same muscle twice in a row, overlapping movements, or a worse movement when a better one is available.

The findings were measured first (below), then corrected on the same branch; the **Corrections** section at the end records what changed and the re-measured rates.

## Method

The pure generator sources (`ProgramGenerator`, `SplitTemplates`, `Types`, `GoalProfiles`, `VolumeModel`, `ExerciseLibrary`, plus the `Equipment` / `ExerciseTag` / `ExercisePlan` definitions from `Program.kt`) were compiled standalone with `kotlinc` and driven by a harness that generated 2,000 weeks per configuration and counted each failure mode. Configurations: full library, a commercial gym set, the frozen MWM-989 preset, dumbbells + bench, plus beginner, `get_stronger`, knees flagged, hips flagged and a pinned isolation. Percentages below come from that run. Example weeks were printed from fixed seeds so every claim can be reproduced.

Already sound and not repeated below: no exercise appears twice in one day; consecutive calendar days never share a prime mover on the default schedule (`CoachingPolicyTest` covers all seven day counts); equipment, dislike, experience and problem-area filters are applied before pins and fallbacks; the manual swap picker excludes movements already in the day.

## Findings

Ordered by how much they degrade a typical plan.

| # | Finding | Evidence | Suggested correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Two deadlift variants in one leg day.** The hamstring HYP slot that follows the hamstring STR hinge is filled by a second hinge far more often than by a leg curl. `PATTERN_REPEAT_PENALTY` (0.25) is applied per candidate, and the library has five hinge candidates against one general leg curl, so the penalised group still out-weighs the alternative. | 38.5% of full-gym leg days on the 5-day split carry two or more hinges; 64.3% on the 6-day split; 25.6% for a commercial gym. Seed 2, 6-day, legs-a: `smith-squat, conventional-deadlift, db-step-up, db-stiff-leg-deadlift`. Seed 3, `get_stronger`, legs: `trap-bar-deadlift, db-romanian-deadlift, pause-squat, conventional-deadlift`. | Give a `MuscleSlot` an optional preferred / avoided pattern (hamstring HYP after a hinge → prefer ISOLATION), or normalise the repeat penalty by the number of candidates sharing the pattern, or drop the multiplier to ~0.05 so one fresh pattern beats five stale ones. |
| 2 | **Near-duplicate isolations in one day.** Every single-joint movement maps to the single `ISOLATION` pattern, which is exempt from the repeat penalty. Two slots of the same muscle therefore often land on the same movement in two flavours. | DB lateral raise *and* cable lateral raise on 24.8% of full-gym push days (16.7% on 6-day). Same-family pairs (barbell + EZ curl, DB + EZ skullcrusher, fly + pec deck) on 8.7% of 5-day days and 11.4% of 6-day days. Seed 4 pinned run, push: `db-fly, db-lateral-raise, cable-fly, cable-lateral-raise, …`. | Add isolation sub-patterns (LATERAL_RAISE, FRONT_RAISE, CURL, TRICEP_EXTENSION, PUSHDOWN, FLY, LEG_CURL, LEG_EXTENSION, CALF_RAISE, CRUNCH, LEG_RAISE, KICKBACK) and apply the repeat penalty to them. |
| 3 | **Heavy quad slot filled by a hinge, leaving a leg day with no squat.** `trap-bar-deadlift` is tagged QUADS with pattern HINGE. Nothing steers the QUADS STR slot toward a squat, and once it hinges the hamstring STR slot must hinge again. | 7.0% of full-gym quad-led days have no SQUAT pattern at all (12.9% on the 3-day split). Seed 1, 5-day, lower: `trap-bar-deadlift, db-stiff-leg-deadlift, barbell-hip-thrust, …`. With knees flagged it is 100%: the only unflagged quad movement is the trap-bar deadlift, so every leg day is three deadlifts (`trap-bar-deadlift, conventional-deadlift, db-single-leg-rdl`). | Slot-level preferred pattern (QUADS STR → SQUAT, HAMSTRINGS STR → HINGE). Re-file the trap-bar deadlift under hamstrings/hinge, or keep it under quads with a low STR bias. |
| 4 | **Heavy slots do not prefer loadable movements.** In a STRENGTH slot every compound with numeric reps gets the same `ROLE_MATCH`, so the barbell row, chest-supported DB row, suspension row and lat pulldown are equally likely leads. The `dbMaxLb` steer only helps when the user has recorded light dumbbells, and only penalises `DUMBBELL` (a kettlebell press is `WEIGHT` and escapes it). Bodyweight movements with numeric reps (`dip`, `suspension-row`) count as fully loadable. | Top heavy-slot leads for a full gym are a flat ~5% each across `chest-supported-db-row`, `suspension-row`, `barbell-row`, `seated-cable-row`, `db-row`, `lat-pulldown`. 33.9–40.7% of heavy slots lead with a dumbbell movement when a barbell is available; 8.6% lead with a bodyweight movement. `get_stronger` prescribes `db-squat 3x4-6` and `machine-chest-press 3x4-6` as often as a barbell lift. | Add a loadability tier factor for STRENGTH slots (barbell / plate-loaded > machine > dumbbell > bodyweight), apply the light-load steer to any non-progressive unit, and treat `dip`, `suspension-row`, `bench-dip` as non-loadable for heavy slots (a `loadable` flag on `ExerciseDef`). Raising `pickBias` on the staple barbell lifts alone is not enough because the tie is structural. |
| 5 | **A pinned isolation takes the heavy slot and evicts the press.** `pinned` is resolved as `candidates.firstOrNull { it.id in params.pinned }` on the *first* slot of that muscle, which is the STRENGTH slot, regardless of scheme. It is also re-placed on every day that trains the muscle. | Pinning `db-fly` on the 5-day split: `db-fly 3x6-10` leads both push and upper; the push day becomes `db-fly, db-lateral-raise, cable-fly, cable-lateral-raise, …` with no press. 25% of heavy slots are isolation-led in that configuration versus 0% otherwise, and same-family duplicates rise to 15.9% of days. | Place a pin in the slot whose scheme best fits its tags (isolation → PUMP, then HYP; compound → STR), honour `usedInWeek` for pins so one pin does not occupy every day, and keep the STR slot's compound filter authoritative. |
| 6 | **Scheme reps overwrite the movement's natural range.** `repsFor` replaces any numeric default with the slot scheme's range. | `leg-press` (10-15) → `6-10`; `conventional-deadlift` (3-6) → `8-12` in a HYP slot; `kb-swing` (12-15) → `8-12`, or `6-8` under `get_stronger`; `pause-squat` (3-6) → `6-8`; `face-pull` (15-20) → `12-15`. | Intersect the scheme range with the movement's natural range, or add a `fixedReps` flag for movements whose range is part of the movement (swings, pause squats, deadlifts). |
| 7 | **Same muscle back-to-back inside a day is written into the templates.** `push()` ends `TRICEPS PUMP, TRICEPS PUMP`; `pull()` opens `BACK STR, BACK HYP, BACK HYP` and ends `BICEPS HYP, BICEPS PUMP`. `OrderingAdvisor` treats exactly this as a defect and, for a non-customised day, *silently reorders the in-memory session* (`DayViewModelRefresh.computeOrderingSuggestion`), so the day list and the live session show different orders. It cannot fix the pull day (three adjacent back compounds are unavoidable in its compound-first block), so that one stays as generated. | 40.0% of days on the 5-day split and 66.7% on the 6-day split have at least one adjacent same-muscle pair, before the advisor runs; 60.0% with knees flagged. Every pull day has three back movements in a row followed by two biceps movements in a row. | Interleave in the templates (push: put a triceps slot between the two shoulder slots; pull: `BACK STR, BACK HYP, REAR_DELTS PUMP, BACK HYP, BICEPS HYP, TRICEPS`-style spacing, or move one biceps slot ahead of the rear-delt work). Alternatively run the advisor's rule inside the generator and persist the result so the plan list matches the session. |
| 8 | **6- and 7-day splits reuse identical templates for A and B days.** The `SplitTemplates` header says repeated-day splits use complementary A/B shapes; that is true for 2- and 4-day only. Both leg days are squat-led, both push days press-led, both pull days shaped the same. Small equipment pools then repeat movements across the week. | 8.7% of placements repeat within the week on the MWM preset, 12.3% on dumbbells + bench, versus ~2% with a full library. | Reuse the existing pattern: `legsB` hinge-led (like `lowerB`), `pushB` overhead-led (SHOULDERS STR first, CHEST HYP), `pullB` row-led versus `pullA` pulldown-led through a slot pattern preference. |
| 9 | **3-day split runs `fullBodyA` twice.** Days A and C are the same template, so glutes, rear delts and calves are trained only on day B. | Intermediate weekly sets on the 3-day split: glutes 3, rear delts 2, calves 2, shoulders 4. Beginner: glutes 2, rear delts 2, calves 2. | A distinct `fullBodyC` (e.g. quad-led with glutes / rear delts / calves as its accessories) so B and C between them cover the small muscles twice. |
| 10 | **Volume trims shave the heavy compound first.** Both the weekly cap and the 24-set session cap remove sets from the *largest* slot; on a tie the first slot in the day wins, which is the STRENGTH slot. Beginners get a flat 2 sets everywhere, so the documented "STRENGTH gets a slightly larger share" never happens (`baseSets` is 3 for STR and HYP alike). | Advanced 5-day legs: `quads/STR=3, hamstrings/STR=3, quads/HYP=4, hamstrings/HYP=4, glutes/HYP=4`. Advanced 6-day pull-b: `back/STR=3, back/HYP=4, back/HYP=4`. Beginner: every slot 2. | Trim in PUMP → HYP → STR order, or weight the trim by scheme; give STR a base of 4 (or +1) so the compound keeps its share after scaling. |
| 11 | **Deload and restore each replace the whole program.** `applyDeloadWeek` / `generateDeloadWeek` and `restoreAfterDeload` call `generate` with a fresh `System.nanoTime()` seed and no persisted seed, so the deload week has different exercises from the program it deloads, and the restore hands back a third program. The `restoreAfterDeload` comment says it keeps the same exercises; it only does so by chance. | Two generations with the same params and different seeds share 3 of 30 slots. | Persist the generation seed with the program (or the picked ids) and reuse it for deload / restore; or implement deload as a set-count scale of the existing program instead of a regeneration. |
| 12 | **Coach stall-swap picks the first library entry, not the best movement.** `ProgressionAdvisor.plateauSuggestion` takes `slot.swapCandidateIds` in library order; `AutoCoachPlanner` applies `candidateIds.first()`. The list is filtered by equipment and dislikes only, ignoring the slot's scheme, movement pattern and the rest of the day. The apply path's `alreadyThere` check only inspects the same slot. | Stalled `back-squat` → "Rotate Back Squat → Goblet Squat". Stalled `db-fly` in a day led by `db-bench-press` → proposes `db-bench-press`. On a commercial-gym 5-day plan the first candidate is already elsewhere in the same day for 11.2% of slots, so an auto-applied swap can put the same movement in a day twice (the manual picker in `DayScreen` guards against this; the coach path does not). | Build swap candidates with the generator's weighting (scheme role, pattern, loadability, `pickBias`) and exclude the day's other exercises before ranking. |
| 13 | **Problem areas are a hard ban, documented as soft.** `ProblemArea` says movements are "strongly down-weighted … soft, not a hard ban"; the generator filters them out. When the filter empties the compound pool, the STRENGTH slot falls through to whatever is left and still prescribes strength reps. | Hips flagged, 5-day: `wall-sit 3x30-60s` leads legs and lower as the heavy quad lift, `lying-leg-curl 3x6-10` is the heavy hamstring lift, one third of heavy slots are isolation-led and two slots per week are dropped. Knees flagged: one quad slot dropped per week and every leg day is three deadlifts (see 3). | Either update the documentation to match, or implement tiers (severe area → exclude, moderate → down-weight) and, when a STRENGTH slot has no compound left, demote it to a HYP / PUMP prescription rather than a 6-10 wall sit. |
| 14 | **Single-day re-roll de-duplicates against a phantom week.** `rerollDay` regenerates a whole week and keeps one day; `usedInWeek` inside that generation refers to the freshly rolled other days, not the unchanged real ones, and the real week only enters as `recent` at 0.25. Set counts are then copied by position, so a dropped slot shifts every later slot's sets onto a different scheme. | With the MWM chest pool (two compounds) a re-rolled push-b can land on the press push-a already uses, because only the phantom push-a is in `usedInWeek`. | Seed `usedInWeek` with the other days' actual ids (a `fixedDays` parameter), and map sets by slot index rather than by exercise position. |
| 15 | **Light-dumbbell steer misses kettlebells and non-dumbbell light loads.** `preferStack` only penalises `ExerciseUnit.DUMBBELL`. | `kb-press` and `kb-goblet-squat` are `WEIGHT` and keep full weight in a heavy slot for a user whose heaviest bell is 25 lb. | Penalise any unit whose available load is below `LIGHT_DB_LB`, or add a per-unit ceiling. |

## Lower-priority notes

- `SplitTemplates` documentation says the 6- and 7-day splits use A/B variation and that STRENGTH slots get a larger set share; neither is true in code (see 8 and 10).
- The 1-day plan has no rear-delt or glute slot; both rely on indirect work.
- `ExerciseLibrary.patterns` is a separate map from the definitions, so a new library entry silently defaults to `ISOLATION` unless the map is also edited (`barbell-hip-thrust` and `mwm-upright-row` are the two COMPOUND-tagged entries that have already fallen through and count as isolations for both the repeat penalty and the recovery guard). A `pattern` field on `ExerciseDef` with a test that every COMPOUND entry has one would close this.
- `TrainingRecovery.muscles` gives the upright row no secondary muscles because it has no pattern, so a pull day following a push day with an upright row is not flagged for shoulders.

## Validation

- Harness: 2,000 seeds per configuration, 13 configurations, against the unmodified production generator sources; example weeks from seeds 1–5 and 10/11.
- Existing suites (`ProgramGeneratorTest`, `CoachingPolicyTest`, `VolumeModelTest`) were read, not re-run: no Android SDK is present in this environment. None of the findings above are covered by an existing assertion; `noSingleMuscleDominatesADay` allows three, `multiDaySplitVariesMovementsAcrossDays` allows 25% repeats, and no test checks adjacency within a day, pattern repeats, loadability of the heavy lead, pin placement, deload continuity or the coach swap target.

## Corrections

Applied on `claude/plan-making-system-review-46wxow` after the review above, and re-measured with the same harness (2,000 seeds per configuration).

| # | Change | Before → after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The pattern-repeat penalty is shared by the whole group of candidates that carry a used pattern (`PATTERN_REPEAT_PENALTY / groupSize`, 0.1 total) instead of applied per candidate, so one fresh leg curl out-weighs five stale hinges. A general `seated-leg-curl` entry was added so the second leg day of a 6-day split has a fresh curl rather than a third deadlift. | Two hinges on a leg day: 38.5% → 9.5% (5-day full gym), 25.6% → 2.4% (commercial), 64.3% → 14.1% (6-day full gym). The residual is mostly a trap-bar deadlift or KB swing landing in an accessory slot. |
| 2 | `MovementPattern` gained single-joint families (FLY, LATERAL_RAISE, REAR_DELT, PULLOVER, CURL, TRICEP_EXTENSION, PUSHDOWN, LEG_EXTENSION, LEG_CURL, HIP_THRUST, KICKBACK, CALF_RAISE); every isolation in the library is filed, and the repeat penalty applies to them. `ExerciseLibraryTest` now fails if a compound has no pattern or an isolation has no family. The push day's first shoulder slot prefers a vertical press so the pump slot is the lateral raise (press + raise, not raise + raise). | DB + cable lateral raise on a push day: 24.8% → 3.1% (full), 24.5% → 1.9% (commercial). Same-family pairs: 8.7% → 1.8% of days (5-day), 11.4% → 3.4% (6-day). |
| 3 | `MuscleSlot.pattern` — an optional preferred pattern per slot (QUADS STR → SQUAT, HAMSTRINGS STR → HINGE, chest STR → HORIZONTAL_PUSH, and the A/B leans below); a candidate of another pattern is multiplied by 0.15, never excluded. | Leg days with no squat pattern: 7.0% → 1.2% (full), 12.9% → 2.9% (3-day). With knees flagged the trap-bar deadlift remains the only unflagged quad movement, so that case is unchanged by design. |
| 4 | Loadability tiers in STRENGTH slots: bar / plate-loaded / stack 1.0, dumbbell or kettlebell 0.6, bodyweight 0.3. The light-dumbbell steer now also penalises kettlebell movements and counts them as handheld when deciding whether a stack alternative exists. | Bodyweight-led heavy slots: 8.6% → 3.9%; dumbbell-led when a barbell exists: 33.9% → 25.4% (full), 40.6% → 28.3% (commercial). A dumbbell RDL or press still leads a fair share of heavy slots on purpose; it is no longer a coin-flip against the bar. |
| 5 | Pins are placed in a pre-pass per day: a compound pin takes the muscle's STRENGTH slot (then its first slot); an isolation pin takes the muscle's last PUMP slot, then its last HYPERTROPHY slot, and never a STRENGTH slot — on a day whose only slot for that muscle is the heavy one, the pin is simply not placed. Pins still pass every filter a slot does. | A pinned DB fly is the push day's second chest movement at 8-12 and the day keeps its press; it no longer appears on the upper day at all. Isolation-led heavy slots in that configuration: 25% → 0%. |
| 6 | `ExerciseDef.fixedReps` keeps a movement's own range regardless of scheme (KB swing, pause squat). An isolation that is left alone in a STRENGTH slot is prescribed the HYPERTROPHY range instead of 6-10 or 4-6. | KB swing is always 12-15; a hip-restricted athlete's wall sit / leg curl no longer carries strength reps. The broader "intersect with natural range" idea was rejected: the library's default ranges are hypertrophy starting points, and intersecting would have undone `get_stronger`. |
| 7 | Push and pull templates interleaved so no two adjacent slots share a muscle: push is chest, shoulders, chest, triceps, shoulders, triceps; pull is back, rear delts, back, biceps, back, biceps. Every template on every day-count is now adjacency-free (`ProgramGeneratorQualityTest`). | Days with a same-muscle back-to-back pair: 40.0% → 0.0% (5-day), 66.7% → 0.0% (6-day). The ordering advisor no longer finds anything to re-sort on a generated day, so the day list and the live session agree. |
| 8 | Real A/B templates for the 6- and 7-day splits: push B is overhead-led (SHOULDERS STR first), pull B row-led (pull A pulldown-led), legs B hinge-led. | Both leg days were squat-led; now one squat-led, one hinge-led. |
| 9 | A distinct `fullBodyC` for the 3-day split: pull-first, squat-led, carrying glutes and calves a second time alongside the arm work. | Intermediate 3-day weekly sets: glutes 3 → 6, calves 2 → 4. |
| 10 | Both the weekly cap and the session cap trim PUMP slots first, then HYPERTROPHY, then STRENGTH (largest slot within a scheme). `MuscleSlot`'s doc now says what the code does. | Advanced 5-day legs: squat 3 / leg extension 4 → squat 4 / leg extension 4 / calves 2. |
| 11 | The generation seed is persisted (`PROGRAM_GENERATION_SEED`). `ProgramRepository.generate(keepPicks = true)` replays it; the coach's deload, Settings' deload and `restoreAfterDeload` all pass it. A fresh generate or re-roll writes a new seed. | Same seed + same inputs reproduces the program exactly: a deload changes set counts only and the restore hands the original plan back (`sameSeedReproducesTheProgramAcrossDeloadAndRestore`). Programs generated before this build have no stored seed and fall back to the old behaviour once. |
| 12 | `ProgramGenerator.rankSwapCandidates` ranks a slot's swap pool by role match, same-pattern-first (a fresh variation), loadability for compounds and `pickBias`, and excludes the day's other movements. `AdaptationRepository` feeds it to the snapshot, so the coach's stall-swap and the pain re-route propose from that order. The manual swap picker is unchanged (it already excluded in-day movements). | Stalled back squat → leg press / hack squat / front squat instead of goblet squat. | 14 | `generate(onlyDay, usedElsewhere)`: a single-day re-roll fills only that day and seeds the week-repeat penalty with the other days' actual movements. `rerollDay` passes the current day's picks as `recent` and the other days as `usedElsewhere`, and only carries set counts across positionally when the day kept its slot count. | Covered by `singleDayRerollAvoidsWhatTheOtherDaysUseAndLeavesThemEmpty`. |

### Validation of the corrections

- `ProgramGeneratorQualityTest` (14 tests) pins every finding above; `ExerciseLibraryTest` gained the two metadata checks.
- The pure `program` package and `domain/schedule` suites were compiled standalone with `kotlinc` against JUnit 4 and run here: 115 tests, 0 failures (the Robolectric-backed `CustomExerciseRegistryTest` and `ProgramGenerationIntentTest` need the Android toolchain and were not run).
- `ProgramRepository`, `AdaptationRepository`, `SettingsRepository`, `PreferencesDataStore` and `SettingsViewModel` changes could not be compiled in this environment (no Android SDK); they were reviewed by diff. The full Gradle run should be the gate before merge.
